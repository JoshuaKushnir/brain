# Glossary

- **Aspect:** A divine interpretation of magic. See [[Aspects]].
- **Attunement:** The bond joining a mortal to an Aspect and its Crown. See [[Attunement]].
- **Crown:** A god possessing unique authority over an Aspect. See [[Crown Gods]].
- **Crownlight:** Divine protection and stabilization granted through worship. See [[Crownlight]].
- **Dark Tolerance:** The depth of Darkness familiar to a soul. See [[Dark Tolerance]].
- **Divine Friction:** Conflict between multiple Crownlights. See [[Divine Friction]].
- **Echo:** The coherent remainder of a dead person's identity in [[Undertide]].
- **Holdfast:** The time a Downed soul remains connected to its body. See [[Downed and Holdfast]].
- **Luminance:** Regenerating resistance to unfamiliar Darkness. See [[Luminance]].
- **Stain:** A lasting alteration caused by Darkness, divine influence, or unstable magic.
