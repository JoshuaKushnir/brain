# Hearth

## Description

Hearth serves a dual function as both one of the twelve [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron Gods]] and a significant region central to the mythic geography of the Nightfall era. As a divine entity, it participates in the formation of the [[twelve-mortal-peoples|twelve mortal peoples]] through [[concepts/survival-covenants|survival covenants]]. Beyond its role in the pantheon, Hearth is identified as a primary location of origin or a focal point for these covenants, grounding the gods' influence in the physical world. This duality suggests that the entity and the region are inextricably linked in the unfolding of mortal history.
