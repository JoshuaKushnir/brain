# Ember

Ember is the Aspect of desire, anger, transformation, appetite, and the willingness to spend oneself for change.

Its Crown is Soryn. Ember techniques emphasize ignition, escalation, explosive commitment, heat, sacrifice, and stronger effects gained by accepting risk.

Ember joins [[Tide]] as Crucible and [[03-projects/Nightfall/90-Archive/Legacy Structure/04-Magic/Void]] as Blackflame through [[Confluences]].
