# Faction Reputation

Faction reputation determines contracts, safe houses, trainers, Anchor access, body recovery, legal standing, bounties, and who recognizes a Death Claim.

Reputation should be contextual rather than a universal morality score. Killing a Blackwake raider can please the Watch, begin a crew feud, and anger a Pact settlement that depended on the same smuggler.
