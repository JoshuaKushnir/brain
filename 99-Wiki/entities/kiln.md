# Kiln

## Description

Kiln is identified as one of the twelve [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron Gods]] within the lore of Nightfall. The name symbolizes themes of fire, creation, and transformation, suggesting a foundational role in the development and survival of mortal peoples. As a symbolic deity, Kiln plays an integral part in the established survival covenants governing the world.
