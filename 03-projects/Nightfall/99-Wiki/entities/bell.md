# Bell

## Description

Bell is one of the twelve Patron Gods who together forged the twelve mortal peoples through a series of survival covenants during the period known as Nightfall. The deity’s role is recorded only as a member of this collective divine group, with no individual myths or attributes detailed in the source material. By participating in the covenantal creation narrative, Bell shares in the foundational authority granted to all Patron Gods over the mortal lineages. The entity’s significance therefore derives from its inclusion in the broader pantheon rather than from distinct actions or stories. Bell is linked to the concepts of [[Patron Gods|Patron Gods]], [[survival covenants|survival covenants]], and the formation of the [[twelve mortal peoples|twelve mortal peoples]].
