# Spore

## Description

Spore is recognized as one of the twelve Patron Gods within the established pantheon. Its domain suggests a biological focus, likely encompassing themes of organic growth, natural decomposition, and the fundamental cycles of life. As a member of the divine collective, it provided essential guidance to mortal peoples during the era known as [[Nightfall]]. The deity is often associated with the structural and transformative aspects of the survival covenants.
