# Moths

The Moths are the people of [[Lamp]], historically rooted in [[Gloam]] and [[The Fen|the Fen]].

## Inherited form

reflective eyes, sensory crests, skin dust that brightens near Darkness. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Curiosity is sacred labor. Dangerous knowledge must have multiple witnesses. Every expedition leaves a light home.

## Death custom

At death, a patterned lamp burns while anyone can accurately describe the dead.

A Moth can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
