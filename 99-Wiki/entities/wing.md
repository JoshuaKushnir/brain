# Wing

## Description

Wing is listed as one of the twelve **Patron Gods** who are credited with establishing the twelve mortal peoples through survival covenants during the mythic period known as Nightfall. This deity’s specific functions are not detailed beyond its inclusion in the roster of deities, indicating that its role is understood as part of the collective divine agency responsible for humanity’s mythic origins. As a member of the patron divine cohort, Wing participates in the covenantal process that shapes mortal cultures and identities. The entity is therefore primarily significant in the context of divine patronage and the foundational myths of the twelve peoples.
