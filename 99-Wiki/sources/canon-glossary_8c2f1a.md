## Core Content

The Canon Glossary functions as an essential reference for the metaphysical and narrative framework of the 'Nightfall' setting. It codifies the laws of existence, defining the mechanics behind magic—referred to as [[concepts/aspects|Aspect]]—and the spiritual defense systems like [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]] and [[concepts/crownlight|Crownlight]]. The document explains how individuals maintain stability against the entropic pressure of [[concepts/darkness|Darkness]] and delineates the post-mortem reality involving [[concepts/echo|Echo]] and the possibility of [[concepts/return|Return]] from the [[concepts/undertide|Undertide]]. 

By providing definitions for survival-focused terminology such as [[concepts/dark-tolerance|Dark Tolerance]], [[concepts/holdfast|Holdfast]], and [[concepts/grip|Grip]], the glossary clarifies the relationship between the divine [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron]] deities and the communal survival of people living within the [[concepts/ring|Ring]]. This authoritative lexicon serves as the foundation for the world's governing laws, ensuring consistency in the struggle between identity and dissolution.

## Main Points

- Magic is interpreted through five distinct divine [[concepts/aspects|Aspect]]s.
- The soul interacts with divine power through pacts like [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]], which grants [[concepts/crownlight|Crownlight]].
- Identity faces constant pressure from [[concepts/darkness|Darkness]], which is resisted by internal survival mechanisms.
- Life and death cycles involve the formation of [[concepts/echo|Echo]]es and the potential for [[concepts/return|Return]] from the afterlife.
- Geographic order is defined by the proximity to the Rise and the surrounding [[concepts/ring|Ring]].
