# Downed and Holdfast

When Health reaches zero, the player normally becomes Downed. Their body still lives, but their soul's connection to it is loosening.

The Holdfast meter measures how long that connection lasts. It is affected by remaining [[Luminance]], Fortitude, wounds, local [[Darkness]], ally proximity, equipment, talents, and [[Crownlight]].

While Downed, a player may crawl slowly, communicate, and use limited distress information. They cannot fight normally. Allies may stabilize, revive, drag, or carry them. Enemies may guard, carry, capture, abandon, or [[Execution|execute]] them.

When Holdfast expires, the character dies naturally and Falls into [[Undertide]].
