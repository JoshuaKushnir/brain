# Darkness

Darkness is an environmental and metaphysical pressure that contests stable identity. It grows stronger as travelers move outward through [[Map of the Rings|the Rings]] or downward toward [[Undertide]].

When local Darkness exceeds a character’s [[Dark Tolerance]], their [[Luminance]] drains. At zero Luminance, Darkness begins proposing alternate bodies, memories, loyalties, and histories.

Darkness is not simply low illumination. A bright room can be deeply Dark if reality inside it has become unstable.
