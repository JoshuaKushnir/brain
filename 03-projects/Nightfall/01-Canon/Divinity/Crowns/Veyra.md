# Veyra

Veyra is the Crown God of [[99-Wiki/concepts/ash]]. Veyra is patient, intimate, and quietly possessive.

## Worship

Disciples pursue the emotional discipline of Ash. Their first [[Attunement]] places [[Crownlight]] in the soul and grants protection against [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness]].

## Divided worship

When a disciple accepts a second Aspect, Veyra records every use of a rival Aspect and may erase teachings she considers hers.

## Story use

Veyra can appear personally, teach, bargain, sulk, compete, or fail. Divine power does not guarantee wisdom or moral authority.
