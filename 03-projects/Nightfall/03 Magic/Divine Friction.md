# Divine Friction

Divine Friction is instability created when multiple Crownlights compete inside one soul.

- Minor Friction slows [[Luminance]] recovery.
- Moderate Friction bleeds one Aspect into another.
- Severe Friction directly consumes Luminance.
- Critical Friction lets a Crown suppress its rival temporarily.

Friction fades through rest, sustained use of one Aspect, or a successful [[Confluences|Confluence]] that demonstrates a truth both gods must acknowledge.
