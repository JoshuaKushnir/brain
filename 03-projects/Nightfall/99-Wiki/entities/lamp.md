# Lamp

## Description

Lamp is one of the twelve **Patron Gods** who participated in the survival covenants that forged the twelve mortal peoples during the Nightfall period.  Its role is defined primarily by membership in this divine collective rather than by any individual myths, deeds, or personality traits.  The covenantal narrative positions Lamp alongside other deities such as Hearth, Hammer, and Wing as agents of humanity’s foundational creation.  Although the source provides no distinct stories about Lamp, its inclusion signals a shared responsibility among the Patron Gods for shaping early human societies.  Understanding Lamp therefore relies on its relational context within the broader mythic framework of the Patron Gods and the covenantal creation myth.
