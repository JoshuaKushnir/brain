# Patrons and Crowns

| Relationship | Patron God | Crown God |
|---|---|---|
| Governs | A people and survival tradition | One Aspect of magic |
| Bond begins | Birth, adoption, First Naming | Chosen Attunement trial |
| Gives | Body traits and inherited gift | Crownlight and Aspect magic |
| Expects | Obligation to people and culture | Exclusive magical devotion |
| Greatest offense | Abandoning the people | Taking another Aspect |

Crowns normally tolerate Patron reverence because ancestry does not place another Aspect in the soul. Race, culture, faction, Aspect, and Oath remain independent character layers.
