# Crownlight

Crownlight is the divine protection placed within a mortal during [[Attunement]]. It stabilizes an [[Aspects|Aspect]], strengthens resistance to [[Darkness]], and joins the disciple to a [[Crown Gods|Crown God]].

A faithful single-Aspect disciple receives the strongest Crownlight:

- Better [[Luminance]] recovery
- Reduced drain beyond familiar conditions
- Stable casting at low Luminance
- Temple access and advanced teachings
- Possible divine aid during [[Death]]

[[Multi-Aspect Attunement]] divides or damages this protection. The original Crown usually cannot remove an Aspect already fused to the soul, but can withdraw blessings, close shrines, withhold capstones, or refuse rescue.
