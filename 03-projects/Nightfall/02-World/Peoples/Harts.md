# Harts

The Harts are the people of [[Hart]], historically rooted in [[Redstep]].

## Inherited form

antlers or horn ridges, strong legs, and trail instinct. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Hunter and hunted are temporary roles. Ambush is fair when signs existed. Waste is sacrilege.

## Death custom

At death, antlers travel the final migration route and remain where the deceased turned back.

A Hart can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
