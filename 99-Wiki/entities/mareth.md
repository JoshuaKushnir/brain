# Mareth

## Description

Mareth is a divine figure serving as a member of the [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]] pantheon. Like other deities within this collective, Mareth presides over a specific [[concepts/aspects|Aspect]] that influences the world. The entity demands that its followers engage in exclusive [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]] as a prerequisite for maintaining their magical connection. Through this spiritual bond, worshippers gain access to power unique to the domain of Mareth.

Mareth is specifically identified as the 'Crown' of the Tide aspect, serving as the central anchor point for the aspect's core themes of continuity and pressure.
