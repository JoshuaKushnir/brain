# Shell

## Description

Shell is listed among the twelve **Patron Gods** who together forged the twelve mortal peoples during the event known as Nightfall. Each Patron God, including Shell, entered a **survival covenant** that bound them to the creation and protection of a specific mortal lineage. The narrative does not provide further individual attributes for Shell, so its significance is primarily defined by its membership in this divine cohort. Shell’s role is therefore understood through its connection to the collective actions of the Patron Gods and the covenantal creation story. The entity is linked to other members of the divine group such as [[Hearth|Hearth]], [[Hammer|Hammer]], [[Wing|Wing]], [[Lamp|Lamp]], [[Kiln|Kiln]], [[Spore|Spore]], [[Root|Root]], [[Glass|Glass]], [[Hart|Hart]], [[Face|Face]], and [[Bell|Bell]].
