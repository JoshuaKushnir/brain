## Core Content

This document outlines the metaphysical system of [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]] within the provided magical framework. Rather than acting as mere elemental building blocks, these are defined as divine interpretations of magic, each under the specific governance of a member of the [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]]. 

The text categorizes several primary forces—including [[99-Wiki/concepts/ash|Ash]], [[99-Wiki/concepts/gale|Gale]], [[concepts/tide|Tide]], [[concepts/ember|Ember]], and [[concepts/void|Void]]—as well as the forbidden, non-lawful [[concepts/marrow|Marrow]]. The document explains that practitioners gain access to these forces through the process of [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]]. Furthermore, it details the interaction between these interpretations, noting that combining multiple forces typically results in [[concepts/divine-friction|Divine Friction]], though rare and harmonious states known as [[concepts/confluence|Confluences]] can occur.

## Main Points

- [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]] are divine interpretations of magic dictated by the [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]], rather than interchangeable elements.
- Six primary forces are established, ranging from those of memory and emotion to the forbidden bodily authorship of [[concepts/marrow|Marrow]].
- [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]] is the required method for a practitioner to access their first aspect.
- The combination of multiple aspects is inherently volatile, creating [[concepts/divine-friction|Divine Friction]], while harmony between them results in rare [[concepts/confluence|Confluences]].
