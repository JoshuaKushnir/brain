# Hearth

Hearth is the Patron of the [[Kin]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Hearth changed mortal bodies so they could survive Vigil and Hearth, granting adaptability and social bonds.

## Character

Hearth is warm, generous, intrusive, and convinced shelter creates obligation. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on shared meals, adoption, guest-right, civic service. Its danger is conformity and ownership disguised as care.

## Divine relations

Hearth may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
