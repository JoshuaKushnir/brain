# Execution

Execution, commonly called a Grip, deliberately forces a Downed soul away from its body.

The action is committed and interruptible. The executor cannot freely block or dodge; meaningful damage, guard break, knockback, or forced movement cancels the attempt.

On completion:

- The victim dies and Falls deeper into [[Undertide]] than through untreated Holdfast loss.
- Field revival becomes impossible.
- The killer gains the Death Claim and becomes spiritually associated with the act.
- Loot, durability, witnesses, factions, bounties, and Oaths may react.
- Recovering the body can still reduce the victim's [[Return]] cost.

An execution is therefore tactically useful but more serious than winning a fight.
