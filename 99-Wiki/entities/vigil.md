# Vigil

## Description

Vigil is a primary geographical anchor point for the Nightfall world. It serves as one of the two central hubs around which the map is radially organized. Its position relative to the world's expansion determines the increase of darkness in the surrounding lands. The state of this location is inextricably linked to the progression of the [[entities/the-rise|The Rise]].
