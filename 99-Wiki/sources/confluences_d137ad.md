# Confluences - Summary

- Original file: [[03-projects/Nightfall/90-Archive/Legacy Structure/04-Magic/Confluences.md]]

## Core Content

This source defines "Confluences" as metaphysical truths shared between two jealous [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]], which mortal practitioners can bridge by dividing their allegiance. Rather than being a simple combination of magical elements, a Confluence is a reconciliation of disparate divine perspectives. By forcing both gods to acknowledge the mortal's perspective, the practitioner can unlock powerful phenomena such as "Deepghost" or "Stormfront." The document outlines a rigorous path for this attainment, emphasizing that success requires possessing both relevant Aspects, surviving the intense state of [[concepts/divine-friction|Divine Friction]], and performing a deed that holds specific significance to both involved deities. This framework transforms how magic is understood, moving from mere elemental output to the active negotiation of divine will.

## Main Points

- A Confluence represents a shared truth between two jealous [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]] that a mortal must force them to acknowledge.
- Unlocking these phenomena requires four distinct steps: possessing two Aspects, surviving [[concepts/divine-friction|Divine Friction]], discovering the shared principle, and performing a meaningful deed.
- Confluences are not simply combinations of damage types; they are deeper, metaphysical intersections that reveal truths about the nature of existence.
- The interaction between specific Aspects (such as [[99-Wiki/concepts/ash|Ash]] and Void, or [[entities/tide|Tide]] and [[99-Wiki/concepts/gale|Gale]]) creates unique manifestations, each tied to a specific principle.
