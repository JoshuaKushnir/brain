# Timeline Expansion (Fracture Era)

Corrected per [[Eidolon]]: the Crown Gods imprisoned Eidolon in the [[Pale Wound]]; this severed the world from Eidolon's former wholeness, and [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness]] is the resulting wound-reaction. The "Crowns are Eidolon's divided pieces" idea is a rare heresy, not the core account — mainstream doctrine (both orthodox and Choir) treats Eidolon as still a single, distinct, currently-imprisoned entity.

## The Scattering (immediately after The First Nightfall)
Communities fled outward, cut off by newly fractured terrain and newly partisan light. Twelve separate groups each encountered a smaller, gentler divine figure — not a Crown, but an aspect of care, craft, or migration. Each gave their patron a witness-name — [[03-projects/Nightfall/02-World/Regions/Hearth]], [[Hammer]], [[99-Wiki/entities/shell]], [[99-Wiki/entities/lamp]], [[Kiln]], [[Spore]], [[99-Wiki/entities/root]], [[99-Wiki/entities/glass]], [[99-Wiki/entities/hart]], [[99-Wiki/entities/shell]], [[99-Wiki/entities/face]], [[99-Wiki/entities/bell]] — describing how they were first encountered. These twelve covenants became the twelve peoples. [[Dawn Anchors]] — pre-Nightfall structures nobody now knows how to build — are discovered scattered across the broken land, still holding pre-Nightfall stability untouched by the Crowns' claims.

## The Keeping (founding of Vigil)
Survivors converge on the largest known Dawn Anchor atop the new plateau of [[The Rise]] and keep literal vigil, watching to see if dawn returns unclaimed. It doesn't, fully — but the watching becomes the city's founding ritual and its name. [[Vigil]] is built around the Anchor; [[03-projects/Nightfall/02-World/Regions/Hearth]] grows at the Rise's foot to feed and supply it.

## The Doctrine Split (rise of orthodoxy and the Choir)
Vigil's priesthood needs an origin story that doesn't implicate the gods it depends on. Orthodox doctrine crystallizes: Eidolon was a threat, the Crowns nobly bound it. A minority who preserve the oldest pre-Nightfall memory — [[The Choir]] — remember it differently: the Crowns did this *to* Eidolon. This is the seed of every Choir/Vigil tension. [[The Watch]] forms in the same era, tasked with the practical problem of Attunement, Darkness, and Undertide breaches regardless of who's theologically correct.

## The Age of Attunement
Early Attunement is chaotic. Several early multi-Aspect disciples cause local catastrophes, which is the origin of [[Divine Friction]] as doctrine and taboo, and why [[Confluences]] are treated as dangerous secret knowledge. [[The Five]] consolidates around this era, treating exclusive single-Crown worship as political legitimacy.

## Bridge to existing Timeline
This expansion ends where [[Timeline]] existing "The Ring Age" section begins (refuge spreading through Hearth, then outward into Gloam, Murk, Night, and the rim). No changes proposed to "The Ring Age" or "The Present."
