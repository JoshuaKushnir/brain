# Multi-Aspect Attunement

Taking a second Aspect is Divided Worship, sometimes called Crown Adultery. It is rare because one Crown already claims the soul and because both gods distrust divided loyalty.

## Possible paths

- Divine poaching by a rival Crown
- Theft from a relic, saint, demigod, or divine wound
- An impossible service that earns reluctant consent
- A forbidden soul-splitting rite
- A temporary crisis pact between two Crowns
- Inheritance from a dying divided champion

## Costs

- Weaker [[Crownlight]]
- Faster [[Luminance]] drain beyond tolerance
- Slower recovery outside secure areas
- Restricted capstone teachings
- Hostile shrines, priests, or divine champions
- [[Divine Friction]] when switching
- More dangerous [[Death]] and split Echoes

One Aspect means mastery and devotion. Two mean transgression and versatility. Three begin an ascension-or-monstrosity storyline rather than a normal build.
