# The Fall

The Fall is the soul’s descent into Undertide after Holdfast ends or a Grip completes.

| Place of death | Typical arrival |
|---|---|
| Vigil / Rise | The Steps |
| Hearth | The Shore |
| Gloam | The Shallows |
| Murk | The Drowned Roads |
| Night | The Deep |
| Rim | The Nameless Deep |
| Dark | Unpredictable |

An execution, zero-Luminance death, or multi-Attunement can deepen or split the Fall.
