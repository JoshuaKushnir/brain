# Eidolon

Before the Pale Wound, Eidolon was the ordering condition that let matter, memory, and identity remain consistent — not a humanoid supreme god, but the bound world-entity whose identity joined land, memory, and possibility into one continuous truth. Mortal souls still express a small regenerative share of that former wholeness as [[Luminance]].

The [[Crown Gods]] imprisoned Eidolon within the [[Pale Wound]], and the act split reality into competing histories. [[Vigil]] survives because [[The Rise]] remains strongly aligned with what Eidolon once was; [[Dawn Anchors]] catch and redistribute that lingering stability rather than manufacturing light from nothing.

The orthodox faith calls Eidolon the cause of Nightfall. [[The Choir]] argues that Eidolon is the world's original self and that the Crowns caused the catastrophe by dividing it. A further, rarer heresy holds that the Crowns are not merely responsible for Eidolon's imprisonment but are the divided pieces of Eidolon itself — a claim mainstream Choir doctrine rejects as too radical, since it would mean Eidolon no longer exists as anything separate to free.

Eidolon rarely speaks as one voice. Its influence appears as memories of events that never happened, roads leading toward alternate lives, and strangers who remember one another incorrectly.
