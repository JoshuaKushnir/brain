# Soryn

## Description

Soryn is recognized as one of the [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]], serving as a primary deity within the theological framework of the world. The deity oversees one of the five fundamental [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]], exerting influence over the magical structure of reality. Followers of Soryn are strictly required to adhere to the practice of exclusive magical devotion. This mandate is governed by the [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]] system, which dictates how the divine connection is maintained.
