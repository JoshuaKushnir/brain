# Implementation Notes

## Existing-system alignment

The lore expects separate Health, Mana, Posture, and Luminance resources; distinct Downed, Carried, Grip, and Execution states; and five Aspect kits.

## Needed data

- Darkness tier and environmental exposure rate.
- Player Dark Tolerance and current Luminance.
- Primary and secondary Attunements.
- Crown favor, Divine Friction, and unlocked Confluences.
- Holdfast state and execution interruption.
- Death Claim records and Return consequences.
- People, homeland, faction, Aspect, and Oath as separate identity fields.
