# Boundaries

## Definition

Boundaries represent the spatial and metaphysical limits enforced by the Void aspect. They serve as the definitive perimeters within which Void effects are contained, effectively sequestering targets from the outside world and maintaining a state of absolute separation and silence.

## Key Characteristics

- **Spatial Enforcement:** Establishes a distinct physical or metaphysical zone where external interaction is restricted.
- **Isolation:** Prevents the transmission of information, matter, or energy across the delineated limit.
- **Maintenance of Silence:** Ensures that the internal state of the bounded area remains undisturbed by external influence, reinforcing the nature of the Void.
- **Target Sequestration:** Used as a method to remove paths of interaction by cutting off a specific point of interest from the surrounding environment.

## Applications

- **Containment:** Locking volatile entities or energies within a localized area to prevent systemic contagion or interference.
- **Strategic Isolation:** Removing a target from the battlefield or a communication network by imposing a metaphysical perimeter.
- **Silent Operations:** Creating localized zones of influence where actions can be performed without detection or interaction with the external system.
