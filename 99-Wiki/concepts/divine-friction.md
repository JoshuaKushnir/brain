# Divine Friction

## Definition

Divine Friction is a challenging metaphysical state encountered by mortals when attempting to access a [[Confluence]]. It represents the inherent resistance or existential danger experienced when a conscious mind attempts to hold two or more conflicting divine truths simultaneously.

Combining these Aspects generates Divine Friction, manifesting as the interference or existential conflict that arises when merging disparate, irreconcilable interpretations of magical reality.
## Key Characteristics

- **Existential Resistance:** The phenomenon manifests as a psychological or spiritual strain that grows in intensity as one approaches a point of divine convergence.
- **Cognitive Dissonance:** Requires the subject to synthesize contradictory metaphysical axioms, often leading to potential mental or physical collapse.
- **Rite of Passage:** Surviving the duration of Divine Friction is an essential, involuntary hurdle that acts as a filter for those seeking to unlock or master a [[Confluence]].
- **Attunement Prerequisite:** A first Aspect must be acquired through Attunement, establishing the necessary capacity for magic interaction that serves as the foundation before Divine Friction manifests.
## Applications

- **Gateway Security:** Acts as a natural defense mechanism for [[Confluence]] sites, ensuring that only those with sufficient resilience can interact with higher-order divine structures.
- **Spiritual Testing:** Often cited in theological studies as the primary method used by the [[Crown Gods]] to judge the worthiness of mortal aspirants.
