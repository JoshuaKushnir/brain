# Witnesses

Witnesses is servants of Last and keepers of death law, operating mainly in all settled rings.

## Public role

Members recover bodies, record final words, confirm executions and Returns.

## Central contradiction

The faction’s core conflict is truthful death against divine or emotional refusal.

## Player-facing functions

Membership can provide contracts, safe houses, trainers, travel access, reputation, equipment, rescue, or PvP authority. Betrayal changes who recognizes [[Death Claims]], who recovers the player’s body, and where [[Luminance]] can be restored safely.
