# Kiln

Kiln is the Patron of the [[Cinders]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Kiln changed mortal bodies so they could survive Cinderwake, granting heat resistance and bodily tempering.

## Character

Kiln is patient, demanding, and controlled. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on apprenticeship, ritual argument, communal furnaces. Its danger is repression and mistaking endurance for health.

## Divine relations

Kiln may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
