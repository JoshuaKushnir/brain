# Glass

## Description

Glass is one of the twelve **Patron Gods** enumerated in the source text, a divine figure credited with participating in the formation of the twelve mortal peoples through **survival covenants** during the epoch known as Nightfall. The source provides no individual mythological details or attributes for Glass; its notability stems solely from inclusion in this pantheon. As a member of this divine group, Glass is linked to the broader cosmology that ties together entities such as [[entities/hearth|Hearth]], [[entities/hammer|Hammer]], [[entities/wing|Wing]], [[entities/lamp|Lamp]], [[entities/kiln|Kiln]], [[entities/spore|Spore]], [[entities/root|Root]], [[entities/hart|Hart]], [[entities/shell|Shell]], [[entities/face|Face]], and [[entities/bell|Bell]]. The entity therefore functions as a component of the collective narrative surrounding the **Patron Gods** and the covenantal creation of humanity.

The entity's name evokes thematic associations with clarity, fragility, and transmutation, providing a symbolic layer to an otherwise abstract divine figure. These qualities potentially distinguish Glass within the pantheon, suggesting specific, albeit unstated, symbolic roles in the formation of the mortal peoples.

The Glass is further identified as having played an essential role in the survival covenants that established the foundational identity of the mortal races within the setting.
