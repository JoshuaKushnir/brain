# Multi-Attunement

A second Aspect is Divided Worship, Crown Adultery, or the Second Attunement. Both Crowns distrust it: the first sees betrayal and the second sees a proven divided heart.

## Rare paths

- Divine poaching by a rival Crown.
- A stolen spark from a relic, saint, or divine wound.
- Impossible service compelling reluctant recognition.
- A forbidden Confluence rite that splits the soul.
- Temporary appointment as a shared champion.

## Costs

- Reduced Luminance regeneration and faster Darkness drain.
- Loss of deepest single-Aspect Forms.
- [[Divine Friction]] during switching.
- Restricted temples and active divine enemies.
- Split [[Echoes]] and harder [[Return]] after death.

A third Aspect causes Crown Rupture and begins an ascension-or-monstrosity arc rather than ordinary build progression.
