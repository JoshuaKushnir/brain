## Core Content

The [[entities/crown-gods]] are a central pantheon consisting of five deities: [[entities/veyra]], [[entities/mareth]], [[entities/soryn]], [[entities/aeral]], and [[entities/orr]]. Each member of this divine group holds dominion over one of the five [[concepts/aspects]]. The document outlines a rigid theological framework regarding how mortals interact with these beings; specifically, it establishes that followers must dedicate themselves to exclusive magical devotion. This state of singular dedication is achieved and maintained through a process known as [[concepts/attunement]]. The source provides a foundational overview of the hierarchy and the behavioral mandates required for those seeking to wield power associated with these specific deities.

## Main Points

- The [[entities/crown-gods]] pantheon is composed of five distinct deities.
- Every god in the pantheon governs exactly one of the five [[concepts/aspects]].
- Magical interaction with any member of the pantheon requires the formal process of [[concepts/attunement]].
- The pantheon strictly enforces a policy of exclusive devotion, meaning a follower cannot be attuned to more than one of the [[entities/crown-gods]] at a time.
