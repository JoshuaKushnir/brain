# Gloam

Gloam is the common name for the Second Ring. It is the last broad belt where direct sunlight still reaches the ground consistently.

The light is low, angled, and unreliable. Long shadows support ambushes, hidden settlements, and the first major [[Attunement]] trials outside Crown-controlled temples.

Gloam is where many citizens first learn that [[Luminance]] is not infinite. Roads remain recognizable, but safety no longer follows them.
