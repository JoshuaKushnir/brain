# Spore

Spore is the Patron of the [[Mourn]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Spore changed mortal bodies so they could survive Mournwood, granting ancestral memory through symbiosis.

## Character

Spore is communal, gentle, and unable to value solitude. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on memory gardens, ancestral testimony, chosen forgetting. Its danger is loss of individuality to inherited consensus.

## Divine relations

Spore may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
