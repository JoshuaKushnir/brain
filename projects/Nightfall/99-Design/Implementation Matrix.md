# Implementation Matrix

This note maps canon to game behavior without forcing every lore idea into the first release.

| System | Canon requirement | Gameplay expression |
|---|---|---|
| [[Luminance]] | Resistance to unfamiliar Darkness | Current, max, regeneration, exposure drain |
| [[Dark Tolerance]] | Acclimation with consequences | Safe threshold and progression tradeoffs |
| [[Attunement]] | Exclusive divine bond | Aspect selection through trials and reputation |
| [[Multi-Aspect Attunement]] | Rare divine betrayal | Quest-gated unlock with permanent costs |
| [[Divine Friction]] | Competing Crownlights | Switching pressure and instability meter |
| [[Downed and Holdfast]] | Soul loosening from body | Downed timer, crawl, revive, carry |
| [[Execution]] | Forced separation | Interruptible Grip and Death Claim |
| [[Return]] | Echo climbs from Undertide | Death sequence and persistent scars |
| [[The Rings]] | Darkness rises outward | Radial world progression with local modifiers |

## Design constraints

- Do not spend Luminance as ordinary spell mana.
- Do not make dual-Aspect a standard early build option.
- Do not make executions consequence-free finishing animations.
- Do not make ring number equal a perfectly fixed difficulty.
- Preserve specialization advantages for loyal single-Aspect characters.
