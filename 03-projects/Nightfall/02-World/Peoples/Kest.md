# Kest

The Kest are the people of [[Wing]], historically rooted in [[The Reaches]].

## Inherited form

feathered crests, hollowed bones, controlled falls, pressure sense. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Maps record wind and season. Imprisonment is spiritual mutilation. Some promises last only until the next landing.

## Death custom

At death, remains are released from a height and the name spoken after they vanish.

A Kest can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
