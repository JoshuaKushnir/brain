# Ash techniques

## Definition

Ash techniques are a specialized magical discipline that prioritizes the subtle manipulation of environment and psyche over overt combat damage. By weaponizing traces of past existence and memory, practitioners create decoys, false trails, and residual magical effects to disorient opponents.

## Key Characteristics

- **Subtle Manipulation**: Favors strategic concealment and psychological misdirection instead of direct power.
- **Trace-Based Logic**: Relies on the concept of "remembered positions" and the residue left behind by entities or events.
- **Strategic Deception**: Focuses on creating false trails and decoys to mislead enemies.
- **Non-Elemental Focus**: Operates independently of traditional elemental magic systems.

## Applications

- **Infiltration**: Creating residual trails to bypass security or misdirect pursuers.
- **Psychological Warfare**: Disorienting adversaries by manifesting illusory traces of past threats.
- **Tactical Concealment**: Cloaking one's current location by mimicking the energy signatures of "past" positions.
