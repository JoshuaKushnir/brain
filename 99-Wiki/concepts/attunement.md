# Attunement

## Description

Attunement is a foundational method of magical practice and a state of alignment required by the [[Crown Gods]], serving as the essential spiritual bridge connecting a practitioner to their chosen deity's essence. Beyond its role as a gatekeeping mechanism for initiating one's magical journey, it functions as the primary process for accessing and channeling the specific properties of a magical aspect. This alignment governs the effectiveness with which a user manifests the nuances and powers of their focus, forming the basis for all higher-level magical interactions. The process remains critical for both establishing an initial connection and enabling the potential for [[Multi-Aspect Attunement]].

## Definition

Attunement is a procedural method of magical practice required by the [[Crown Gods]]. It functions as the foundational spiritual bridge connecting an individual follower to the specific essence and power of their chosen deity.

Furthermore, Attunement acts as the critical gatekeeping mechanism for initiating one's magical journey, as the first Aspect is obtained exclusively through this spiritual process.

Additionally, Attunement facilitates the presence of Crownlight within the soul, acting as a formal covenant that ensures stability against external metaphysical pressures.
## Key Characteristics

- **Exclusive Devotion**: Requires the practitioner to commit their magical and spiritual focus to a single deity.
- **Divisional Restriction**: Followers are strictly forbidden from dividing their spiritual attention or magical capacity among multiple Crown Gods.
- **Sacred Requirement**: It acts as the mandatory prerequisite for accessing divine magical capabilities.
- **Crownlight Integration**: Enables the presence of divine protection and aspect stability within the soul.
## Applications

- Establishing a formal pact or connection between a mortal and a deity.
- Channeling specific magical energy granted by one of the Crown Gods.
- Sustaining the link necessary to manifest the [[Aspects]] associated with a deity's influence.
- Stabilizing the soul against external metaphysical pressures such as Darkness.
- In gameplay, Attunement is expressed through aspect selection that is gated by trials and reputation systems, ensuring specialization advantages for loyal single-Aspect characters.
