# Confluence

## Definition

A Confluence is a metaphysical phenomenon representing an immutable truth shared between two [[Crown Gods]]. It serves as a forced acknowledgment of divine reality imposed upon a mortal, manifesting as the intersection of two distinct elemental [[Aspects]].

## Key Characteristics

- **Divine Intersection**: Requires the simultaneous influence of two unique elemental forces.
- **Mortal Impact**: Functions as a catalyst that forces mortal consciousness to reconcile with higher divine truths.
- **Specific Principles**: Categorized into distinct manifestations such as Deepghost or Crucible, depending on the participating elements.
- **Conditionality**: Unlocking a Confluence requires surviving the ordeal of [[Divine Friction]] and performing significant moral or spiritual actions.
- **Non-Additive Nature**: It is not a simple combination of damage types, but a singular, higher-order ontological reality.
- **Composite Manifestations**: Through Confluences, Void combines with elemental aspects such as Ash, Gale, and Ember to form distinct, stable manifestations like Deepghost, Riftwind, and Blackflame.
## Applications

- Used by individuals to anchor divine power within a mortal framework.
- Acts as a milestone for spiritual development, forcing growth through the survival of intense metaphysical stress.
- Provides specialized capabilities that emerge from the synergy of two disparate divine aspects.
