# Marrow

## Definition

Marrow is a forbidden [[Aspect]] associated with the concept of bodily authorship. It is defined by its exclusion from the official magical doctrine established by the [[Crown Gods]], marking it as a transgressive or heretical force within the metaphysical framework of the world.

## Key Characteristics

- **Bodily Authorship**: It pertains to the fundamental control, creation, or rewriting of physical existence and corporeal form.
- **Forbidden Status**: It is explicitly unrecognized and prohibited by the [[Crown Gods]].
- **Extra-Doctrinal**: It operates outside the lawful and regulated systems of magic, suggesting it does not derive its power from traditional divine sources.

## Applications

- Used in arcane rituals that seek to alter or transcend natural physical limitations.
- Employed by practitioners operating in opposition to established religious or magical authorities.
- Theoretical studies regarding the limits of divine influence over biological matter.
