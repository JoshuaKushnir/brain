# Eidolon

## Description

Eidolon is a primary cosmological entity that serves as a central pillar in the project's world-building, existing alongside the [[entities/pale-wound|Pale Wound]] and [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness|Darkness]]. It has recently incorporated the concept of the [[concepts/radiance|Radiance]], marking a significant shift in its metaphysical status. While historical theories attempted to link the entity to 'Crowns', those interpretations have since been relegated to the status of heresy. It functions as a foundational reference point for navigating the history and underlying metaphysical framework of the setting.
