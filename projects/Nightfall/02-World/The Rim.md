# The Rim

The Rim is the Fifth Ring and the final belt that can be mapped with any consistency. Residents commonly call it the Edge, rejecting [[Vigil]]'s implication that their homeland exists only as civilization's boundary.

Geography drifts, routes repeat incorrectly, and graves cannot always be trusted to remain where they were dug. The dead are tied to named objects so their identities have a stable reference.

Beyond the Rim begins [[The Dark]]. Beneath it, routes into [[Undertide]] approach the living world.
