# Glass

## Description

Glass is one of the twelve **Patron Gods** enumerated in the source text, a divine figure credited with participating in the formation of the twelve mortal peoples through **survival covenants** during the epoch known as Nightfall. The source provides no individual mythological details or attributes for Glass; its notability stems solely from inclusion in this pantheon. As a member of this divine group, Glass is linked to the broader cosmology that ties together entities such as [[Hearth|Hearth]], [[Hammer|Hammer]], [[Wing|Wing]], [[Lamp|Lamp]], [[Kiln|Kiln]], [[Spore|Spore]], [[Root|Root]], [[Hart|Hart]], [[Shell|Shell]], [[Face|Face]], and [[Bell|Bell]]. The entity therefore functions as a component of the collective narrative surrounding the **Patron Gods** and the covenantal creation of humanity.
