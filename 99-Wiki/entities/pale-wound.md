# Pale Wound

## Description

The Pale Wound is one of the three finalized cosmological proper nouns that define the setting's metaphysical framework. It maintains a distinct identity within the revised structure following the consolidation of the setting's cosmology. As a fundamental element of reality, it functions as a primary feature of the world's existence alongside entities such as the [[entities/eidolon|Eidolon]] and [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness|Darkness]]. Its presence suggests a deep, ontological scar within the fabric of the universe.

Situated within the causal chain of the universe, it occupies the position between the primordial ordering condition and the subsequent emergence of [[concepts/darkness|Darkness]] as a reactive phenomenon. This placement highlights its critical function in the metaphysical transition from initial order to the manifestation of decay.
