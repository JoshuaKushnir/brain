# Patron Gods

Patron Gods are divine ancestors, exemplars, adopters, or transformers of Nightfall's peoples. Their worship influences culture, inherited traits, aesthetics, social duties, and sometimes physical form.

A Patron is not automatically weaker than a [[Crown Gods|Crown]]. Their authorities differ: Crowns govern [[Aspects]], while Patrons are strongest when protecting their people, homeland, defining craft, or sacred tradition.

A person may honor a Patron while being Attuned to a Crown, but competing obligations can create story conflicts. Patron intervention may also offer a path through [[Return]] when a Crown refuses to help a divided disciple.

Each playable people should eventually receive a dedicated note containing its Patron, homeland across [[The Rings]], funeral customs, visual language, internal factions, and relationship to [[Vigil]].
