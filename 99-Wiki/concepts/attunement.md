# Attunement

## Definition

Attunement is a procedural method of magical practice required by the [[Crown Gods]]. It functions as the foundational spiritual bridge connecting an individual follower to the specific essence and power of their chosen deity.

Furthermore, Attunement acts as the critical gatekeeping mechanism for initiating one's magical journey, as the first Aspect is obtained exclusively through this spiritual process.
## Key Characteristics

- **Exclusive Devotion**: Requires the practitioner to commit their magical and spiritual focus to a single deity.
- **Divisional Restriction**: Followers are strictly forbidden from dividing their spiritual attention or magical capacity among multiple Crown Gods.
- **Sacred Requirement**: It acts as the mandatory prerequisite for accessing divine magical capabilities.

## Applications

- Establishing a formal pact or connection between a mortal and a deity.
- Channeling specific magical energy granted by one of the Crown Gods.
- Sustaining the link necessary to manifest the [[Aspects]] associated with a deity's influence.
