# Exploration Loop

1. Leave [[Vigil]] and travel outward or downward.
2. Enter Darkness beyond current [[Dark Tolerance]].
3. Spend [[Luminance]] while navigating hazards and false routes.
4. Fight through Health, Mana, and Posture pressure.
5. Become [[Downed]] if defeated.
6. Depend on rescue, mercy, preparation, or [[Holdfast]].
7. Experience [[The Fall]] if death occurs.
8. Navigate a [[Return]] path or accept a bargain.
9. Return with scars, debts, Echoes, or changed relationships.
10. Prepare differently before descending again.
