# Faction

## Definition

A faction is a collective group within a game world that functions as a primary social or political unit, characterized by shared objectives, distinct territories, specific roles, and internal organizational structures. The Faction Template serves as a structural design tool to define these core attributes, ensuring a standardized approach to world-building and narrative consistency.

## Key Characteristics

- **Distinct Identity**: Possesses a unique ideology, cultural set, or goal that separates it from other groups.
- **Internal Structure**: Features defined hierarchies, ranks, or governance systems.
- **Territoriality**: Occupies or asserts control over specific geographic or digital domains within the game world.
- **Relational Complexity**: Engages in diplomatic, hostile, or neutral interactions with other factions and individual players.
- **Social Influence**: Acts as a driver for narrative development and gameplay progression through its status and reach.
- **Standardized Schema**: Enforces consistent documentation of lore and mechanics, harmonizing disparate game data into cohesive entity profiles.

## Applications

- **Narrative Building**: Used to create political tension and storylines involving power struggles or alliances, serving as a reference for designers to maintain consistency in quests and dialogue.
- **Game Mechanics**: Serves as a framework for reputation systems, quest lines, and player alignment, while providing a mechanical basis for developers to align attributes with systems.
- **World Simulation**: Acts as an entity that reacts to player choices, dynamically altering the state of the game world.
- **Content Pipeline**: Streamlines the onboarding of new content by providing a rigid structure for lore creation.
