# Echo

## Definition

An Echo is a metaphysical phenomenon representing the remnant of an individual's identity that persists in reality following physical death. It serves as a manifestation of remembrance, allowing a person's essence to continue influencing the world beyond the expiration of their biological form.

## Key Characteristics

- **Persistence**: Exists independently of the physical body.
- **Metaphysical Nature**: Classified as a phenomenon of remembrance within the framework of reality.
- **Influence**: Acts as a post-mortem extension of an individual's persona or legacy.
- **Distinctiveness**: Differentiates the surviving essence of the self from the deceased physical vessel.

## Applications

- **Legacy Preservation**: Used to describe how historical figures or powerful beings continue to exert pressure or guidance on living populations.
- **Metaphysical Research**: Serves as a primary object of study for understanding the boundary between life and death.
- **Spiritual Interaction**: Contextualizes encounters with non-physical remnants of deceased individuals.
