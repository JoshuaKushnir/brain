# Ash

Ash is the Aspect of what remains: memory, traces, regret, concealment, and the lies people preserve to survive.

Its Crown is Veyra. Ash techniques should emphasize marks, delayed effects, false trails, decoys, remembered positions, and residue rather than generic fire damage.

Ash joins [[Void]] as Deepghost and [[Tide]] as Mistveil through [[Confluences]].

See [[Crown Gods]], [[Attunement]], and [[Multi-Aspect Attunement]].
