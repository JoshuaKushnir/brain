# Darkness

## Description

Darkness is one of the three core pillars of the project's cosmology. Following recent structural consolidation, it now encompasses the heresy line known as [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness|The Unlit]]. Its role in the setting's causality has been explicitly corrected to define it as a reactive force rather than the origin of the world's broken state. It maintains a complex relationship with other fundamental powers, such as the [[entities/eidolon|Eidolon]] and the [[entities/pale-wound|Pale Wound]].
