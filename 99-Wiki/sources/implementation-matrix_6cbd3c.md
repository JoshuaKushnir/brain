## Core Content

The [[concepts/implementation-matrix|Implementation Matrix]] acts as the primary architectural document for the Nightfall project, bridging the gap between narrative canon and functional gameplay. It systematically outlines nine core mechanics, including [[concepts/luminance|Luminance]], [[concepts/dark-tolerance|Dark Tolerance]], and [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]], ensuring that lore-driven requirements are translated into meaningful, balanced game systems. 

The document establishes essential design constraints to maintain mechanical integrity. Notably, it prohibits the conversion of [[concepts/luminance|Luminance]] into general-purpose mana and mandates that advanced progression features, such as [[concepts/multi-aspect-attunement|Multi-Aspect Attunement]], remain gated and costly to protect the value of single-aspect specialization. By defining specific gameplay expressions for abstract lore concepts—such as the [[concepts/holdfast|Downed and Holdfast]] survival mechanics or the tactical, high-risk nature of [[concepts/grip|Execution]]—the matrix ensures that systemic depth remains consistent with the game's thematic tension.

## Main Points

- The matrix provides a direct mapping between canon requirements and game implementation to ensure lore consistency.
- [[concepts/luminance|Luminance]] is strictly restricted from functioning as standard spell mana to preserve its survival-oriented utility.
- [[concepts/multi-aspect-attunement|Multi-Aspect Attunement]] is discouraged for early-game play to preserve the mechanical significance of character specialization.
- Combat mechanics like [[concepts/grip|Execution]] are designed to be high-risk, tactical maneuvers rather than simple finishing animations.
- World difficulty, scaled through [[concepts/ring-order|The Rings]], is intended to be fluid and influenced by local modifiers rather than a fixed linear progression.
