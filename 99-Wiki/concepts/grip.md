# Grip

## Definition

Grip refers to the committed execution of an action or intent. It serves as a measure of an individual's agency and decisiveness, representing a state of active, sustained engagement with one’s environment and a firm hold over one’s own faculties.

## Key Characteristics

- **Resolve**: A manifestation of internal determination and focus.
- **Agency**: Reflects the capacity to exert influence or maintain control over external circumstances.
- **Active Engagement**: Denotes a transition from passive observation to deliberate, committed interaction with the world.
- **Stability**: Implies a sense of "holding on" to a specific course of action or state of mind.

## Applications

- **Self-Regulation**: Used to describe the act of maintaining focus on cognitive or emotional goals.
- **Strategic Implementation**: Applied in scenarios where decisive action is required to steer outcomes in a desired direction.
- **Agency Assessment**: Serves as a diagnostic metric for how effectively an individual manages their interaction with reality.
