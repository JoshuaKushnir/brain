# Face

## Description

Face is one of the twelve **Patron Gods** who are said to have formed the twelve mortal peoples through **survival covenants** during the Nightfall period. The source material provides no further details about Face’s appearance, personality, or specific domains, so its primary significance stems from its inclusion among the divine patrons. As a member of this divine cadre, Face is linked to the broader mythological framework that explains humanity’s covenantal creation. The entity therefore serves as a connective node between the pantheon of Patron Gods and the concept of covenant‑based formation of peoples.
