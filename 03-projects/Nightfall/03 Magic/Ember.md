# Ember

Ember is the Aspect of desire, anger, escalation, and transformation. Its Crown is [[Soryn]].

In combat, Ember emphasizes aggressive commitment and stack-based escalation. Loyal single-Attuned disciples gain the deepest stability; divided disciples risk [[Divine Friction]].

See [[Attunement]], [[Crownlight]], and [[Confluences]].
