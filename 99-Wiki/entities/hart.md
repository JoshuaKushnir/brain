# Hart

## Description

Hart is identified as one of the twelve [[Patron Gods|Patron Gods]] who together established the twelve mortal peoples through survival covenants during the Nightfall epoch. As a member of this divine collective, Hart’s role is defined primarily by its participation in the covenantal process that created humanity. No additional attributes or narratives about Hart are provided in the source material, leaving its identity closely tied to the broader concept of the twelve patron deities. The entity is linked to other elemental or thematic entities such as [[99-Wiki/entities/hearth|Hearth]], [[Hammer|Hammer]], and [[99-Wiki/entities/wing|Wing]] within the mythic taxonomy.
