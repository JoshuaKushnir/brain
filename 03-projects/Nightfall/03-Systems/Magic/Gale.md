# Gale

Gale is the Aspect of motion, freedom, defiance, and displacement. Its Crown is [[Aeral]].

In combat, Gale emphasizes aerial control, mobility, and zone disruption. Loyal single-Attuned disciples gain the deepest stability; divided disciples risk [[Divine Friction]].

See [[Attunement]], [[Crownlight]], and [[Confluences]].
