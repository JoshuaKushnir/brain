# Tide

Tide is the Aspect of pressure, continuity, grief, and debt. Its Crown is [[Mareth]].

In combat, Tide emphasizes resource denial, terrain control, sustained pressure. Loyal single-Attuned disciples gain the deepest stability; divided disciples risk [[Divine Friction]].

See [[Attunement]], [[Crownlight]], and [[Confluences]].
