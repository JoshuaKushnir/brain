# The Watch

The Watch is Vigil's military and civil authority, operating mainly in Vigil and Hearth.

## Public role

Members protect roads, register Attuned, investigate executions, control travel.

## Central contradiction

The faction’s core conflict is security becoming surveillance, conscription, and central ownership.

## Player-facing functions

Membership can provide contracts, safe houses, trainers, travel access, reputation, equipment, rescue, or PvP authority. Betrayal changes who recognizes [[Death Claims]], who recovers the player’s body, and where [[Luminance]] can be restored safely.
