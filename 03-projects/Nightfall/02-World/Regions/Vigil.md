# Vigil

Vigil is the central city and political heart of the mapped world. It crowns [[The Rise]], gathering around an ancient Dawn Anchor that catches sunlight and redistributes it through mirrors, channels, and sacred architecture.

The city took its name from the survivors who remained awake during the first [[entities/nightfall]], waiting to see whether dawn would return. "Keep vigil" is therefore a prayer, civic duty, and warning.

Vigil is safer than the rings, but not morally pure. Elevation determines wealth: temples and ruling houses occupy the sunlit heights, while laborers, lifts, cisterns, and refugees crowd the lower terraces.

Vigil claims to protect civilization. Its enemies claim it monopolizes light.
