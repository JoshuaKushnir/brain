# Combat Resources

Nightfall's major player resources should remain distinct in both function and fiction.

| Resource | Function |
|---|---|
| Health | Bodily survival |
| Posture | Balance, guard integrity, and vulnerability to knock |
| Mana | Immediate cost of ordinary magical techniques |
| [[Luminance]] | Resistance to unfamiliar [[Darkness]] |

Health reaching zero normally leads to [[Downed and Holdfast|Downed]], not immediate death. Posture pressure creates openings. Mana limits spell output. Luminance governs how long exploration remains safe and how coherent the character remains during [[Death]].
