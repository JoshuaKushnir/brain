# Darkness

Darkness is the pressure by which the world disputes established identity and history. It grows broadly stronger outward across [[The Rings]] and downward toward [[Undertide]].

Darkness does more than reduce visibility. It alters sound, paths, wildlife, memory, causality, and what people believe has always been true.

A character within familiar exposure regenerates [[Luminance]]. Exposure beyond [[Dark Tolerance]] drains it. Depth, weather, broken Anchors, divine activity, and local wounds can make a place darker than its ring suggests.

Darkness is not [[99-Wiki/concepts/void]] and not [[Death]]. Void separates; death concludes; Darkness revises.
