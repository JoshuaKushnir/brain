# Ash

## Description

Ash is an elemental aspect that engages in a [[concepts/confluence|Confluence]] with the Void, resulting in the manifestation of Deepghost. Governed by one of the [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]], this aspect is fundamentally associated with the thematic properties of memory, residue, deception, and regret. Within magical systems, Ash serves as a bridge between divine interpretation and the combinatory nature of magic, such as when it combines with [[entities/tide|Tide]] to manifest as Mistveil. Its intersection with the Void embodies the truth that 'What is forgotten approaches absence,' while its pairing with Tide reflects that 'Water preserves traces while mist conceals them.' Through these interactions, Ash illustrates how specific elemental forces define the metaphysical consequences of magic within the established framework.
