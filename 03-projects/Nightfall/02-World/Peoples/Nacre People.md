# Nacre People

The Nacre People are the people of [[99-Wiki/entities/shell]], historically rooted in [[Nacre]].

## Inherited form

pearlescent skin, pressure resistance, limited air-water breathing. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Homes and names have layers. Rescue swimming comes before overland travel. Councils close like shells.

## Death custom

At death, a memory pearl is placed in a communal reef.

A Nacre People can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
