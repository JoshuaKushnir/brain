## Core Content

The source defines a pantheon called the **Patron Gods**, a collective of twelve divine beings: [[entities/hearth|Hearth]], [[entities/hammer|Hammer]], [[entities/wing|Wing]], [[entities/lamp|Lamp]], [[entities/kiln|Kiln]], [[entities/spore|Spore]], [[entities/root|Root]], [[entities/glass|Glass]], [[entities/hart|Hart]], [[entities/shell|Shell]], [[entities/face|Face]], and [[entities/bell|Bell]]. These deities are credited with creating the **twelve mortal peoples** through a process described as **survival covenants** during the epoch known as Nightfall. The text functions as a concise mythic definition, linking the divine roster to the foundational origin of human societies in this lore.
