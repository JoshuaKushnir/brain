# Gale

## Description

Gale represents the fundamental force of wind and atmospheric motion. It serves as a core element that interacts with [[entities/tide|Tide]] and [[entities/void|Void]] to manifest complex phenomena such as [[entities/stormfront|Stormfront]] and [[entities/riftwind|Riftwind]]. Symbolically, it is linked to the concept of movement and the expansive nature of the horizon. It acts as a bridge between atmospheric stability and active change.
