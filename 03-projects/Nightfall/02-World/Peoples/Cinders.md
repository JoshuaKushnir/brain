# Cinders

The Cinders are the people of [[Kiln]], historically rooted in [[Cinderwake]].

## Inherited form

heat-resistant skin and lines that glow with strong emotion. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Anger is material to shape rather than suppress. Failure calls for a masterwork, not an apology alone.

## Death custom

At death, bones are vitrified and incorporated into the communal furnace.

A Cinder can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
