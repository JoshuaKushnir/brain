# Confluences

Confluences are not automatic dual damage types. They are new magical behaviors discovered where two jealous Aspects share a defensible truth.

| Pair | Confluence | Shared truth |
|---|---|---|
| [[Ash]] + [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Void]] | Deepghost | What is forgotten approaches absence |
| [[Tide]] + [[Gale]] | Stormfront | Sea and sky meet at a moving horizon |
| [[Ash]] + [[Tide]] | Mistveil | Water preserves traces while mist hides them |
| [[Ash]] + [[Ember]] | Cinderwake | A concealed ember becomes delayed consequence |
| [[Ember]] + [[Tide]] | Crucible | Opposition creates pressure and transformation |
| [[Ember]] + [[Gale]] | Sunscour | Motion feeds heat and commitment |
| [[Ember]] + [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Void]] | Blackflame | Hunger can consume even magic |
| [[Gale]] + [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Void]] | Riftwind | Movement requires empty space |
| [[Tide]] + [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Void]] | Abyssal | Depth isolates through pressure |

Unlocking one requires both Aspects, survival of Friction, discovery of the shared principle, and a deed meaningful to both Crowns.
