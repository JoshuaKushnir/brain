# Cosmology

## Definition

The field of cosmology defines the metaphysical, historical, and structural framework of the game's world. It encompasses the fundamental nature of existence, the origins of the environment, and the overarching principles governing reality.

## Key Characteristics

- **Structural Foundation**: Acts as the blueprint for the game's reality and its causal relationships.
- **Ongoing Refinement**: Subject to iterative updates, specifically regarding the consolidation of core pillars.
- **Abstraction**: Focuses on abstract principles rather than specific granular narratives, though it informs all narrative output.
- **Internal Consistency**: A primary goal of the field is to ensure that all world elements remain logically aligned under the established metaphysical pillars.

## Applications

- **World-building**: Providing the foundational rules for lore development and character backstories.
- **Narrative Design**: Guiding the creation of historical events and the manifestation of supernatural or physical phenomena within the setting.
- **Consistency Audits**: Used as a reference point to simplify and consolidate complex or contradictory lore elements.
