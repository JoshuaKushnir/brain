# Crownlight

Crownlight is the divine flame placed in a soul during [[Attunement]]. It stabilizes an [[Aspects|Aspect]], protects [[Luminance]], and connects the disciple to one of the [[Crown Gods]].

The Crown expects exclusive worship. A second Crownlight turns divine protection into competition and creates [[Divine Friction]].
