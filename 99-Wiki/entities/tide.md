# Tide

## Description

Tide is an aspect representing fluid, cyclical, or directional forces, often associated with water or natural movement. It serves as a vital component in multiple confluences, including those with [[entities/gale|Gale]], [[concepts/ash|Ash]], and [[entities/ember|Ember]]. It is characterized by its capacity to preserve, transform, and influence the environment it traverses. As a foundational force, it dictates the rhythm of change within its sphere of influence.
