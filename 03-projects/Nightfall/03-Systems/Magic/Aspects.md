# Aspects

The five Aspects are [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Ash]], [[Tide]], [[Ember]], [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Gale]], and [[03-projects/Nightfall/03-Systems/Magic/Void]]. Each is governed by one of the [[Crown Gods]] and stabilized in a mortal soul through [[Crownlight]].
