# Hart

Hart is the Patron of the [[Harts]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Hart changed mortal bodies so they could survive Redstep, granting endurance and trail instinct.

## Character

Hart is proud, fair by harsh standards, and intensely competitive. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on migration, hunts, warnings, reciprocal pursuit. Its danger is honor violence and cruelty disguised as fairness.

## Divine relations

Hart may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
