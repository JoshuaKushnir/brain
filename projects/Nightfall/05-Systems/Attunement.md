# Attunement

Attunement is the sacred bond through which a [[Crown Gods|Crown God]] joins a mortal to one of the [[Aspects]]. It is discipleship, adoption, ownership, and worship at once—not learning from a trainer menu.

Each Crown tests a defining quality:

- Veyra: understanding regret and concealed truth
- Aeral: choosing freedom over safety
- Mareth: carrying grief and obligation
- Soryn: turning desire into decisive change
- Orr: accepting absence without filling it recklessly

The first bond grants [[Crownlight]]. A loyal single-Aspect disciple gains deep specialization, efficient [[Luminance]] protection, shrine access, and the Crown's strongest teachings.
