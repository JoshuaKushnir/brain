# Power Tiers

Power tiers describe maximum influence, not automatic victory. Domain, preparation, worship, Luminance, Darkness, Oaths, geography, and matchups matter.

| Tier | Examples |
|---|---|
| S+ | [[Radiance]], [[The Unlit]] as foundational conditions |
| S | [[Eidolon]], the five [[Crown Gods]] |
| A | [[Patron Gods]], greater leviathans, four-Aspect catastrophes |
| B | Demigods, ascended saints, Crown avatars, three-Aspect beings |
| C | Master Attuned, dual-Aspect Confluents, greater Nightspawn |
| D | Veteran Attuned, faction champions, expert delvers |
| E | Newly Attuned, soldiers, ordinary people |

A Patron can rival a Crown while defending its people or covenant. A leviathan can overpower a divine avatar physically without gaining authority over Attunement.
