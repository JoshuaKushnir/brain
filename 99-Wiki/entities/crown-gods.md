# Crown Gods

## Description

The Crown Gods are powerful, jealous divine entities who govern the fundamental aspects of reality. They serve as the originators of the shared truths known as [[concepts/confluence|Confluences]], which they compel mortals to acknowledge and honor. Their interaction with the mortal realm is strictly transactional, requiring individuals to perform meaningful deeds to unlock divine secrets. These entities typically manifest in pairs or oppositional groupings, forming the structural framework that dictates the nature of magical reality. They are often perceived as orchestrators of [[concepts/divine-friction|Divine Friction]], using their influence to shape the trajectory of history. The pantheon consists of five specific deities: [[entities/veyra|Veyra]], [[entities/mareth|Mareth]], [[entities/soryn|Soryn]], [[entities/aeral|Aeral]], and [[entities/orr|Orr]]. Each of these gods governs one of the five [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]] of the world and demands exclusive magical devotion from their followers, a requirement established through the process of [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]].
