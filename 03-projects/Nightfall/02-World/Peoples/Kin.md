# Kin

The Kin are the people of [[03-projects/Nightfall/02-World/Regions/Hearth]], historically rooted in [[Vigil]] and [[03-projects/Nightfall/02-World/Regions/Hearth]].

## Inherited form

adaptable bodies and unusually strong cooperative bonds. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Kin culture absorbs foreign words, tools, food, and institutions quickly. It also tends to claim ownership after incorporation.

## Death custom

At death, an empty place at the deceased's favorite meal; eating from it accepts unfinished duty.

A Kin can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
