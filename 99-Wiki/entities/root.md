# Root

## Description

Root is one of the twelve **Patron Gods** who are said to have forged the mythic origins of the twelve mortal peoples during the Nightfall era. According to the source, each Patron God entered into a survival covenant that bound the newly created peoples to the divine order, and Root’s role is defined solely by its membership in this divine cohort. The entity does not receive an individual narrative beyond this collective function, making its significance primarily relational within the pantheon. As a member of the Patron Gods, Root is linked to the broader covenantal framework that shapes humanity’s mythic heritage. The entity’s name is preserved in its original form without translation.
