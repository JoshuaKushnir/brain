# Undertide

## Definition

Undertide is a term for a geographical or metaphysical reflection of the rings of the world, consisting of seven distinct layers that correspond to each of the seven rings.

## Key Characteristics

- **Seven-Layered Structure**: The Undertide mirrors the world's structure through seven distinct levels.
- **Hierarchical Depth**: Each layer corresponds to a specific ring in the physical or metaphysical world.
- **Categorized Levels**:
    1. The Steps
    2. The Shore
    3. The Shallows
    4. The Drowned Roads
    5. The Deep
    6. The Nameless Deep
    7. Unpredictable

## Applications

- Used to describe the underlying metaphysical reflections of world structures.
- Serves as a framework for navigating or understanding the depth and layer-based reality of the rings.
