# The Five

The Five is institution joining the five Crown temples, operating mainly in Vigil and inner rings.

## Public role

Members control legal Attunement, shrines, Aspect teaching, and stolen-spark prosecutions.

## Central contradiction

The faction’s core conflict is religious monopoly and covert rivalry between temples.

## Player-facing functions

Membership can provide contracts, safe houses, trainers, travel access, reputation, equipment, rescue, or PvP authority. Betrayal changes who recognizes [[Death Claims]], who recovers the player’s body, and where [[Luminance]] can be restored safely.
