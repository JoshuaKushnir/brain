# The Dark

The Dark is not simply the Sixth Ring. It is the region beyond reliable geography, measurement, and historical agreement.

Darkness there does not only injure intruders. It proposes alternatives: another road that was always taken, another face an ally always had, another past that feels equally true.

Natural [[Luminance]] recovery is unreliable. Dawn Anchors may fail, point in impossible directions, or attract the things they are meant to repel.

The Dark is endgame territory, a source of forbidden truth, and the place where permanent identity loss becomes possible.
