# Soryn

Soryn is the Crown God of [[Ember]]. Soryn is passionate, competitive, and destructive when bored.

## Worship

Disciples pursue the emotional discipline of Ember. Their first [[Attunement]] places [[Crownlight]] in the soul and grants protection against [[Darkness]].

## Divided worship

When a disciple accepts a second Aspect, Soryn forces divided disciples to prove they can carry both flames.

## Story use

Soryn can appear personally, teach, bargain, sulk, compete, or fail. Divine power does not guarantee wisdom or moral authority.
