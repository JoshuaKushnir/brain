# Darkness

## Description

Darkness is one of the three core pillars of the project's cosmology. Following recent structural consolidation, it now encompasses the heresy line known as [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness|The Unlit]]. Its role in the setting's causality has been explicitly corrected to define it as a reactive force rather than the origin of the world's broken state. It maintains a complex relationship with other fundamental powers, such as the [[entities/eidolon|Eidolon]] and the [[entities/pale-wound|Pale Wound]].

It also functions as the final boundary in the ring-based geography of the world, marking the threshold beyond which physical existence ceases. This spatial placement underscores its function as the ultimate container for the world's internal processes.

Furthermore, Darkness is defined as a pressure that actively contests the stability of an individual's identity. This functional metaphysical detail highlights its role in challenging the coherence of sentient beings within the world's internal processes.
