## Core Content

This document establishes the foundational narrative constraints and design philosophies for the [[concepts/canon-glossary|Canon Decisions]] of the Nightfall project. It mandates a radial world-building structure centered on [[entities/vigil|Vigil]] and [[entities/the-rise|The Rise]], where darkness increases moving outward. The text defines the functional parameters for core mechanics, clarifying that [[concepts/luminance|Luminance]] is an exploration and identity resource rather than a traditional mana pool, and that [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]] require exclusive worship. By implementing a high-cost system for [[concepts/multi-aspect-attunement|Multi-Attunement]] and a multi-stage process for [[concepts/death|Death]], the project ensures internal consistency and narrative weight. Furthermore, the document enforces negative constraints to prevent common genre tropes, such as ensuring [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron Gods]] cannot assign [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]], thereby maintaining a clear boundary between mortal creation and divine power.

## Main Points

- The map is radial around [[entities/vigil|Vigil]] and [[entities/the-rise|The Rise]], with darkness increasing outward.
- [[concepts/luminance|Luminance]] functions as an exploration and identity resource rather than a magic pool.
- Magical worship dedicated to [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]] must be exclusive.
- [[concepts/multi-aspect-attunement|Multi-Attunement]] is intentionally designed as a difficult, costly, and story-defining mechanic.
- [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron Gods]] are responsible for creating peoples but are restricted from assigning [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]].
- [[concepts/death|Death]] is defined by a multi-stage process (Downed, Carry, Grip, Fall, Echo, Return) to preserve narrative consequence.
- The project intentionally avoids tropes like consequence-free respawns and locked racial classes.
