# Return

Return is the process by which a dead Echo climbs from [[Undertide]] back toward its body or another valid point of restoration.

Possible routes include:

- Following a Dawn Anchor
- Ally recovery of the body
- Aid from a loyal [[Crown Gods|Crown God]]
- Aid from a [[Patron Gods|Patron God]]
- A bargain with [[Last]]
- A forbidden door carrying Stain, memory loss, or mutation

High [[Luminance]] at death produces a clearer route. Low Luminance creates false paths and hostile Echoes. Death at zero Luminance allows Darkness to present competing versions of the character.

[[Multi-Aspect Attunement|Multi-Attuned]] souls are pulled in different divine directions and may need to reconcile or reunite Aspect-specific Echoes before returning.
