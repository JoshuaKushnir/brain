# World Naming Ideas

Decision (2026-09-17): **Nightfall stays the name of the event**, not the world. If the world itself needs a separate proper noun (distinct from the event and from [[Vigil]]/[[The Rings]] as the inhabited-region name), these are candidate names inspired by [[Eidolon]]'s imprisonment:

- **Sundered Eidolon** / **The Sundering** — names the founding catastrophe directly.
- **Crownfall** — pun on Nightfall, points at the Crown Gods' act rather than just the dimming sun.
- **Shardlight** — ties the [[Crownlight]] magic system's name to the imprisonment imagery.
- **The Riven** — short, usable as both world-name and in-world proper noun ("born of the Riven," "before the Riven").

Recommended layering if adopted: **World name → event name (Nightfall) → Eidolon/Crown mythology → regional Ring names**, matching the way Tolkien separates "the Fall" from "Middle-earth" as distinct naming layers.

Status: not locked. No world-name has been chosen yet beyond keeping "Nightfall" as the event.
