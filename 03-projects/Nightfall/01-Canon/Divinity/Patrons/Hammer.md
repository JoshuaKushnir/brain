# Hammer

Hammer is the Patron of the [[Irons]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Hammer changed mortal bodies so they could survive Ironroot, granting dense bones and powerful posture.

## Character

Hammer is blunt, protective, and unable to admit a design failed. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on craft, repair, tools, construction as proof. Its danger is stubborn hierarchy and preservation of failed traditions.

## Divine relations

Hammer may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[Bell]] when death customs are violated.
