# Night

Night is the Fourth Ring. The name is literal: most low ground receives no ordinary daylight, even when the sun is visible from distant peaks.

Communities maintain death-lamps, body watches, and strict expedition customs. Leaving a corpse unattended risks corruption, false resurrection, or the growth of Nightspawn.

[[Crownlight]] becomes strategically important here, which makes [[Multi-Aspect Attunement]] especially dangerous: divided worship provides more tools but weaker divine protection.
