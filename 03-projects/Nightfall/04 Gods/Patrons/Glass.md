# Glass

Glass is the Patron of the [[Facets]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Glass changed mortal bodies so they could survive Meridian, granting light storage and refraction.

## Character

Glass is beautiful, exact, and vain. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on visible obligation, contracts, controlled imperfection. Its danger is perfectionism and public judgment.

## Divine relations

Glass may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[Bell]] when death customs are violated.
