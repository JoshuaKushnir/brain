# Holdfast

Holdfast is the temporary attachment between a Downed character’s soul and body. It weakens with time, low [[Luminance]], local Darkness, severe wounds, and failed rescue.

If Holdfast empties, the person experiences [[The Fall]]. A successful [[Grip]] bypasses the remaining attachment.
