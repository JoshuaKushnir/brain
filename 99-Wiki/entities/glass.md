# Glass

## Description

Glass is one of the twelve **Patron Gods** enumerated in the source text, a divine figure credited with participating in the formation of the twelve mortal peoples through **survival covenants** during the epoch known as Nightfall. The source provides no individual mythological details or attributes for Glass; its notability stems solely from inclusion in this pantheon. As a member of this divine group, Glass is linked to the broader cosmology that ties together entities such as [[99-Wiki/entities/hearth|Hearth]], [[Hammer|Hammer]], [[99-Wiki/entities/wing|Wing]], [[99-Wiki/entities/lamp|Lamp]], [[Kiln|Kiln]], [[Spore|Spore]], [[99-Wiki/entities/root|Root]], [[99-Wiki/entities/hart|Hart]], [[99-Wiki/entities/shell|Shell]], [[99-Wiki/entities/face|Face]], and [[99-Wiki/entities/bell|Bell]]. The entity therefore functions as a component of the collective narrative surrounding the **Patron Gods** and the covenantal creation of humanity.
