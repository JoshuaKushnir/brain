# Canon Glossary

- **Aspect:** one of five divine interpretations of magic.
- **Attunement:** pact that places Crownlight in the soul.
- **Crownlight:** divine protection and Aspect stability.
- **Darkness:** pressure that contests stable identity.
- **Dark Tolerance:** familiar exposure before Luminance drains.
- **Echo:** identity reality remembers after death.
- **Grip:** committed execution.
- **Holdfast:** temporary bond between Downed soul and body.
- **Luminance:** regenerating resistance to unfamiliar Darkness.
- **Patron:** god of a people and survival covenant.
- **Return:** passage from Undertide back to life.
- **Ring:** broad geographic belt around the Rise.
