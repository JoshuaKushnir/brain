# Tide

Tide is the Aspect of continuity, pressure, grief, debt, and everything carried forward.

Its Crown is Mareth. Tide techniques emphasize flow, pull, redirection, pressure, defensive shaping, and effects that accumulate rather than vanish.

Tide joins [[Gale]] as Stormfront, [[Ash]] as Mistveil, and [[Ember]] as Crucible through [[Confluences]].
