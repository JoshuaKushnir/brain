# mobility

## Definition

Mobility is a core combat concept defined by the capacity for displacement and spatial control. Within the context of Gale-based techniques, it involves the active manipulation of a character's position and the surrounding battlefield geometry to secure a tactical advantage.

Displacement is a specific manifestation of Gale's influence, serving as a tactical magical technique used to force movement or knockback opponents. It represents the active mechanical expression of Gale's core tenet of refusal to remain confined.
## Key Characteristics

- **Spatial Control**: The ability to dictate the physical distance between combatants.
- **Dynamic Displacement**: The capacity for fluid movement across the battlefield.
- **Tactical Freedom**: Prioritizing the removal of movement constraints to maintain battlefield initiative.
- **Geometry Manipulation**: Integrating environmental features into one's positional strategy.

## Applications

- **Distance Management**: Maintaining an optimal range to exploit opponent weaknesses while staying outside their effective reach.
- **Positioning**: Repositioning to avoid incoming attacks or to better align for offensive maneuvers.
- **Battlefield Control**: Utilizing movement to force opponents into unfavorable zones or environmental hazards.
