# Vigil Culture

Vigil treats height, light, registration, and continuity as civic virtues. Households keep lamps for absent relatives; government offices record lineage, Crownmarks, deaths, and travel permits.

Its strength is coordination at enormous scale. Its danger is turning shelter into ownership and stability into surveillance.

[[The Watch]] and [[The Five]] dominate public life, while [[The Lamps]] challenge the idea that central authority owns distributed light.
