# Gods MOC

## Crown Gods

[[Veyra]], [[Mareth]], [[Soryn]], [[Aeral]], and [[Orr]] govern the five [[Aspects]]. They demand exclusive magical devotion.

## Patron Gods

[[Hearth]], [[Hammer]], [[Wing]], [[Lamp]], [[Kiln]], [[Spore]], [[Root]], [[Glass]], [[Hart]], [[Shell]], [[Face]], and [[Bell]] created inherited survival lineages during the first Nightfall.

## Common god

[[Last]] guards death, final words, inheritance, and truthful endings.

See [[Patrons and Crowns]] for the distinction.
