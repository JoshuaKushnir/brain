# Last

Last is the common god of death, final words, burial, mercy, inheritance, and accurate remembrance.

Tradition claims Last became divine by remaining beside the first dying mortal when every other god turned away. Last does not own every soul, but witnesses when a life has truly ended.

Last opposes false resurrection because it replaces continuity with imitation. The central teaching is:

> Death keeps the name. Darkness changes it.

Every people portrays Last differently. Many funerals leave one empty seat for the final witness.

Last may permit [[Return]] in exchange for unfinished business, but repeated resurrection eventually draws judgment.
