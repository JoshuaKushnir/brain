# Root

Root is the Patron of the [[Groves]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Root changed mortal bodies so they could survive The Gardens, granting regrowth and land sense.

## Character

Root is loving, ancient, and possessive. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on cultivation, reciprocity, migration with the land. Its danger is treating departure as betrayal or amputation.

## Divine relations

Root may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[Bell]] when death customs are violated.
