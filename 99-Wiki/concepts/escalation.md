# Escalation

## Definition

Escalation is a core methodological component of Ember-based magic, representing the process of increasing intensity after the initial ignition. It facilitates the transition from a stable state to a high-energy, transformative state.

## Key Characteristics

- Requires continuous commitment of additional resources or risks to maintain magical flow.
- Acts as a bridge between stable initial ignition and high-energy release.
- Essential for achieving high-risk, high-reward magical outcomes.
- Characterized by the amplification of power density within an Ember-based system.

## Applications

- Scaling magical output during combat or high-pressure situations.
- Achieving complex transformative states that require sustained power growth.
- Pushing the boundaries of established magical stability for experimental purposes.
