# Returned

The Returned are the people of [[99-Wiki/entities/bell]], historically rooted in scattered enclaves.

## Inherited form

Undertide markings and sensitivity to weak boundaries. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Near-death can begin a new legal life. Bells distinguish completed death from expected Return.

## Death custom

At death, one bell rings for each life claimed; the final ring belongs to Last.

A Returned can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
