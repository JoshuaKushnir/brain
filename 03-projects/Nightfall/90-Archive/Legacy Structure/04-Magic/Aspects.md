# Aspects

Aspects are divine interpretations of magic rather than interchangeable elements. Each is governed by one of the [[Crown Gods]].

- [[03-projects/Nightfall/90-Archive/Legacy Structure/04-Magic/Ash]]: memory, residue, deception, regret
- [[03-projects/Nightfall/90-Archive/Legacy Structure/04-Magic/Gale]]: movement, freedom, defiance, direction
- [[Tide]]: continuity, pressure, grief, obligation
- [[Ember]]: desire, anger, transformation, expenditure
- [[03-projects/Nightfall/90-Archive/Legacy Structure/04-Magic/Void]]: restraint, separation, silence, absence
- [[Marrow]]: forbidden bodily authorship; not recognized as a lawful Crown Aspect

A first Aspect comes through [[Attunement]]. Combining Aspects creates [[Divine Friction]], while rare reconciliations produce [[Confluences]].
