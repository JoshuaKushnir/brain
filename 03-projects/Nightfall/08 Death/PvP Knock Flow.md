# PvP Knock Flow

When two attackers knock one member of an enemy pair, the knock creates a control objective rather than an instant kill.

## Attackers

- Pressure the remaining enemy and create a temporary two-on-one.
- Guard the body to deny rescue.
- Attempt an interruptible [[Grip]].
- [[Carry]] the victim away for ransom, bounty, or separation.
- Leave without killing to avoid a Death Claim.

## Defenders

- Fight for space and attempt a field revive.
- Drag or carry the ally behind cover.
- Interrupt Grip with damage, guard break, knockback, or displacement.
- Retreat and abandon the rescue.

The Downed player crawls, communicates, and signals while [[Holdfast]] decreases. Ambient Darkness continues to affect [[Luminance]].
