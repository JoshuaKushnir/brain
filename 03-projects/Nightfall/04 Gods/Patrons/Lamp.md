# Lamp

Lamp is the Patron of the [[Moths]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Lamp changed mortal bodies so they could survive Gloam and the Fen, granting dark sight and Luminance sensing.

## Character

Lamp is curious beyond compassion. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on questions, expeditions, shared dangerous knowledge. Its danger is fatal curiosity and treating followers as instruments.

## Divine relations

Lamp may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[Bell]] when death customs are violated.
