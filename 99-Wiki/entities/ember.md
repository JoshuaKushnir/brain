# Ember

## Description

Ember represents the fundamental aspect of heat, transformation, and the act of consuming energy. It serves as a primary force that interacts with [[entities/tide|Tide]] and [[entities/void|Void]] to manifest the powerful confluences known as [[entities/crucible|Crucible]] and [[entities/blackflame|Blackflame]]. The nature of Ember is intrinsically linked to the concepts of intensity, primal hunger, and perpetual change. It is often regarded as a catalyst for destruction and renewal within the systems it influences.
