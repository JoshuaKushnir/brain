# Ash

## Description

Ash is an elemental aspect that engages in a [[concepts/confluence|Confluence]] with the Void. This interaction results in the manifestation of a state or entity referred to as Deepghost. Within the context of magical systems, Ash serves as a fundamental illustration of how distinct elemental forces combine to form new properties. Its primary function is to define the combinatory nature of magic through its relationship with the Void.
