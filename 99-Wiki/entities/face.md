# Face

## Description

Face is one of the twelve [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron Gods]] who facilitated the survival of the mortal peoples through [[concepts/survival-covenants|survival covenants]] during the Nightfall period. While the entity's specific domains remain obscure, its name suggests a potential association with human expression, identity, or physical characteristics. As a core member of this divine cadre, Face functions as a primary subject in the study of how the world's diverse populations originated. It serves as a vital connective node between the established pantheon and the covenant-based formation of humanity.

Known also by the title "The Face," the deity is central to the historical understanding of how the mortal races were established. It remains a key focus for researchers studying the ancient origins of the world's peoples.
