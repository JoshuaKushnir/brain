# Multi-Aspect Death

Multiple Crownlights pull a dead Echo in different directions.

- Return costs more Luminance.
- One Crown may demand surrender of the rival Aspect.
- The Echo may split into Aspect-specific selves.
- Repeated deaths can make the split permanent.
- Three Aspects risk Threefold Death, with Echoes scattered across Undertide.

[[The Split]] specializes in recovering and reconciling divided Echoes.
