# World Shape

The inhabited world is organized around [[Vigil]], a city built atop [[The Rise]]. The Rise is so high that its upper city reaches above much of the cloud cover and receives the world's most reliable sunlight.

Around it lie [[The Rings]]. Ambient [[Darkness]] generally increases with distance from the center. Depth also matters: caves beneath [[Hearth]] can hold conditions normally associated with [[Murk]] or [[Night]], while a high peak near [[The Rim]] may briefly receive safer light.

The central rule is:

> Height approaches light. Distance and depth approach the Dark.

The radial world creates the primary expedition loop: prepare near the center, travel outward or downward, spend [[Luminance]], recover knowledge and materials, then return before the Darkness rewrites too much.
