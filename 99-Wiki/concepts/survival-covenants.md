# survival covenants

## Definition

Survival covenants are foundational agreements or bonds established between the [[Patron Gods]] and the twelve mortal peoples, specifically instituted during the era of the [[Nightfall]] to guarantee the continued existence of mortal life.

## Key Characteristics

- **Origin**: Established during the apocalyptic events of the [[Nightfall]].
- **Purpose**: To provide supernatural protection and sustenance to mortals when their extinction was imminent.
- **Contractual Nature**: Represent a formal, binding obligation between divine entities and mortal civilizations.
- **Theological Significance**: They define the relationship between the divine and the mortal, serving as a pillar of the world's belief systems.

## Applications

- **Protection**: Serving as a shield or buffer against the catastrophic magical or environmental collapse of the [[Nightfall]].
- **Societal Governance**: Dictating the religious and ethical frameworks adopted by the twelve mortal peoples to honor their end of the bargain.
- **History**: Used as a temporal anchor to divide history into pre-covenant and post-covenant eras.
