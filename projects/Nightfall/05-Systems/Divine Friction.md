# Divine Friction

Divine Friction is the instability produced when multiple Crownlights compete within one soul.

Friction rises through repeated switching, invoking opposed divine values, failed [[Confluences]], low-[[Luminance]] casting, and acts that openly favor one Crown over another.

Effects escalate from slowed Luminance recovery to mixed ability behavior, direct Luminance loss, Stain, involuntary Aspect suppression, and a Crown briefly seizing control of its own power.

Friction fades through rest, meditation, sustained commitment to one Aspect, divine reconciliation, or correctly performed Confluences. This rewards deliberate sequences rather than constant ability cycling.
