# Tide techniques

## Definition

Tide techniques are a specialized methodology characterized by the manipulation of flow and pressure. This approach prioritizes the redirection of forces and the application of defensive shaping, favoring mechanics that persist or accumulate over time rather than relying on transient actions.

## Key Characteristics

- **Persistence**: Focuses on mechanics that build, maintain, or accumulate effects over an extended duration.
- **Defensive Shaping**: Employs structural redirection of force to manage incoming pressure.
- **Flow Manipulation**: Utilizes the properties of pull and pressure to control the environment or the state of an interaction.
- **Debt-Oriented**: Operates through the management of continuity and delayed consequences rather than instantaneous resolution.

## Applications

- Managing sustained environmental or kinetic pressure in complex scenarios.
- Defensive positioning where force is redirected away from the practitioner.
- Long-term tactical setups that rely on the gradual accumulation of, or transition into, the [[Tide]] aspect.
