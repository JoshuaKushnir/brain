# Holdfast

## Definition

Holdfast is a transient, binding connection that bridges a soul to its physical body when that individual has been "Downed." It functions as an emergency tether, preventing the permanent departure or detachment of the soul during moments of fatal trauma or near-death defeat.

## Key Characteristics

- **Transient Nature**: It is an inherently temporary state that cannot be sustained indefinitely.
- **Emergency Stabilization**: Acts as a spiritual safety mechanism to preserve the connection between consciousness and physical form.
- **Dependency**: Requires the entity to be in a "Downed" state to manifest.
- **Survival Utility**: Provides a critical, short-term window for potential recovery or external intervention.

## Applications

- **Survival Preservation**: Used by beings to avoid complete soul detachment following combat defeat.
- **Emergency Mitigation**: Provides a buffer period for restorative measures to be applied before the soul fully separates from the body.
