# Pale Wound

The Pale Wound is the visible prison of [[Eidolon]] in the sky. Darkness descends through it and settles most heavily far from [[The Rise]] and in low places.

Its phases change routes, [[Luminance]] drain, [[Echoes]], and the reliability of maps. During a Wound flare, even [[Hearth]] can briefly experience conditions associated with [[Murk]].
