# Irons

The Irons are the people of [[Hammer]], historically rooted in [[Ironroot]].

## Inherited form

compact heavy bodies, dense bones, mineral lines around old wounds. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

A leader must have built something still useful. An apology is incomplete without repair. Tools carry inheritance.

## Death custom

At death, the name is struck into the deceased's most-used tool.

A Iron can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
