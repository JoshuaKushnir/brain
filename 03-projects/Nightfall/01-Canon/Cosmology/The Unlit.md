# The Unlit

The Unlit is the absence before form and recognition. It is not evil, conscious, or identical to [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Cosmology/Darkness]]. Darkness is the world’s wounded reaction to separation from [[Radiance]] and the imprisonment of [[Eidolon]].

The deepest heresies claim the Unlit is restful and that existence itself is the wound.
