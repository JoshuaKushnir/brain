# Separation

## Definition

Separation is a functional property of the Void that enables the decoupling of entities or connections. It serves as the mechanism by which the Aspect of the Void interrupts pathways of interaction, effectively isolating targets from their surrounding environment.

## Key Characteristics

- **Decoupling**: The act of severing existing links or causal pathways between entities.
- **Isolation**: Ensuring that a target exists independently of its surrounding context.
- **Boundary Maintenance**: A foundational tool for establishing and preserving distinct boundaries within the Void.
- **Interruption**: The ability to actively prevent interaction or integration between disparate elements.

## Applications

- Establishing absolute silence by severing acoustic or energetic pathways.
- Preventing the integration of foreign elements into a specific domain.
- Creating isolated operational environments where outside interference is nullified.
- Defining strict perimeters to maintain structural or conceptual stability.
