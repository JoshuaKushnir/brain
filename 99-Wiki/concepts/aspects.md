# Aspects

## Definition

The Aspects refer to the five fundamental domains or cosmic forces that underpin the reality of the world, each of which is governed by one of the Crown Gods. They serve as the primary conduits through which divine influence and magical energy permeate the physical realm.

## Key Characteristics

- **Divine Stewardship**: Each of the five deities maintains exclusive authority over a single, unique Aspect.
- **Fundamental Nature**: They represent the essential forces of existence rather than mere elements or attributes.
- **Mediated Access**: Interaction with these forces typically requires adherence to specific religious, ritualistic, or magical protocols established by the pantheon.

## Applications

- **Theological Worship**: Serves as the structural basis for the religious rites performed by followers of the Crown Gods.
- **Magical Theory**: Provides the framework for understanding and channeling power, often requiring [[Attunement]] to bridge the gap between the mortal practitioner and the divine source.
- **Cosmological Order**: Defines the boundaries of influence for each deity, maintaining the balance of the world.
