# Ash

Ash is the Aspect of memory, regret, deception, and hidden truth. Its Crown is [[Veyra]].

In combat, Ash emphasizes misdirection, patience, information advantage. Loyal single-Attuned disciples gain the deepest stability; divided disciples risk [[Divine Friction]].

See [[Attunement]], [[Crownlight]], and [[Confluences]].
