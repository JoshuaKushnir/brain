# Groves

The Groves are the people of [[99-Wiki/entities/root]], historically rooted in [[The Gardens]].

## Inherited form

barklike patches, plant growth, and rapid recovery in healthy soil. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Ownership comes from care. Criminal justice often requires restoring damaged ecosystems.

## Death custom

At death, the dead are grafted into living memorial trees.

A Grove can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
