# Timeline

## Before Nightfall

The world possessed wider daylight, older names, and routes now preserved only in ruins. The [[Crown Gods]] and [[Patron Gods]] competed through peoples, miracles, and early Attunements.

## The First Nightfall

A divine catastrophe wounded the relation between light, world, and memory. The center rose into [[The Rise]] while outer lands darkened, fractured, or sank toward [[Undertide]]. Survivors kept watch for dawn and founded [[Vigil]].

## The Ring Age

Refuge spread through [[03-projects/Nightfall/02-World/Regions/Hearth]], then outward into [[Gloam]], [[Murk]], [[Night]], and [[The Rim]]. Governments numbered the rings; common people named them through lived experience.

## The Present

Dawn Anchors fail, Darkness advances irregularly, Crowns compete for complete disciples, forbidden [[Multi-Aspect Attunement]] spreads, and expeditions search the outer world for the cause of Nightfall and the force beneath the Rise.
