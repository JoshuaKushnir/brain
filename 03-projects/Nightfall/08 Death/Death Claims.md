# Death Claims

A Death Claim links killer, victim, method, witnesses, place, and lawful context. It governs bounty proof, inheritance disputes, faction response, and loot rights.

A Claim does not summon guards automatically. [[The Watch]], [[Witnesses]], [[The Pact]], and [[Blackwake]] recognize and trade different forms of proof.
