# History

## Definition

The History concept refers to the narrative or factual background of an entity's emergence and development within the [[entities/nightfall]] project. It serves as a documentation layer for the provenance and developmental trajectory of an entity, connecting its current state to its inception or evolution over time.

## Key Characteristics

- **Chronological Documentation**: Tracks the progression of an entity from its initial conception to its current state.
- **Contextual Anchoring**: Provides the necessary background to understand why an entity exists in its present form.
- **Decision Tracking**: Records the iterative changes and design decisions that have shaped an entity throughout the project life cycle.
- **Logical Traceability**: Enables contributors to reconstruct the evolution of ideas and features relative to the wider [[entities/nightfall]] framework.

## Applications

- **Contextualizing Entities**: Essential for maintaining an understanding of how an entity fits into the broader world of the project.
- **Developmental Auditing**: Used by contributors to review previous iterations and understand the rationale behind past design shifts.
- **Project Governance**: Facilitates knowledge transfer and ensures consistency by preserving the rationale behind entity modifications.
