# Aeral

## Description

Aeral is one of the five [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]] tasked with maintaining order in the known realms. This deity is specifically responsible for governing one of the five [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]], serving as a central pillar of cosmic structure. Practitioners seeking to channel divine influence must undergo the rigorous process of [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]] to bridge the gap between mortal and divine. Such a connection requires a state of exclusive magical devotion to Aeral, ensuring that the practitioner's essence remains aligned with their specific domain.

Aeral serves as the anchor for the [[99-Wiki/concepts/gale|Aspect of Gale]], which governs mobility, displacement, air pressure, and precise aerial control. This domain makes Aeral the essential guardian of movement and atmospheric stability across the known realms.
