# Ignition

## Definition

Ignition is a foundational technique associated with the Ember aspect, representing the initial spark of energy required for magical manifestation. It serves as the primary catalyst for escalation and explosive commitment in magical practice.

## Key Characteristics

- **Catalytic Initiation**: Acts as the essential starting point for the manifestation of latent power.
- **Transformative Risk**: Inherently linked to the philosophy of expending one's own resources to catalyze change.
- **Escalation Trigger**: Serves as the first step in a sequence that leads to explosive or high-intensity magical outcomes.
- **Ember Alignment**: Specifically bound to the operational principles of the Ember aspect.

## Applications

- Used by practitioners to initiate the process of magical transformation.
- Employed as a "trigger" for more complex, multi-stage magical sequences that require a sudden release of energy.
- Utilized in scenarios where high-risk, high-reward commitment is necessary to alter a magical state.
