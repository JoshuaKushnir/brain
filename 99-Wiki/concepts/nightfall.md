# Nightfall

## Definition

Nightfall is a catastrophic historical event characterized as a period of profound global instability and existential challenge, serving as the definitive transition point between the era of the ancients and the emergence of the twelve mortal peoples.

## Key Characteristics

- Represents a global era of extreme hardship and systemic collapse.
- Acted as the catalyst for the direct intervention of the [[Patron Gods]].
- Serves as the temporal foundation for the establishment of the [[survival covenants]].
- Marked by the dissolution of previous societal structures and the subsequent rise of the twelve mortal races.

## Applications

- Used by historians to define the "Pre-Nightfall" and "Post-Nightfall" chronological eras.
- Referenced in theological studies to justify the necessity of divine protection and the origin of mortal obligations to the divine.
- Serves as the cultural and legal framework for interpreting the validity and historical necessity of the [[survival covenants]].
