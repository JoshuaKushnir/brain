# Darkness

Darkness is an environmental and metaphysical pressure that contests stable identity. It is the world's wounded reaction to the Crown Gods imprisoning [[Eidolon]] and severing the world from Eidolon's former wholeness — not a being, and not evil or conscious in itself. It grows stronger as travelers move outward through [[Map of the Rings|the Rings]] or downward toward [[Undertide]].

When local Darkness exceeds a character's [[Dark Tolerance]], their [[Luminance]] drains. At zero Luminance, Darkness begins proposing alternate bodies, memories, loyalties, and histories.

Darkness is not simply low illumination. A bright room can be deeply Dark if reality inside it has become unstable.

The deepest heresies go further still, claiming that the absence beneath Darkness is restful rather than hostile, and that existence itself — not just Eidolon's imprisonment — is the wound.
