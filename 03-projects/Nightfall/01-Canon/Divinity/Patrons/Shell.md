# Shell

Shell is the Patron of the [[Nacre People]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Shell changed mortal bodies so they could survive Nacre, granting water adaptation and pressure resistance.

## Character

Shell is protective to the point of imprisonment. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on layered names, rescue training, closed councils. Its danger is isolation and fear of the outside.

## Divine relations

Shell may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
