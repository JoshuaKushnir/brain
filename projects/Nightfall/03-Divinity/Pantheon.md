# Pantheon

Nightfall's gods are powerful personalities bound to peoples, forces, places, and obligations. They do not form a clean bureaucratic ladder.

## Major groups

- [[Crown Gods]] possess unique authority over the five legal [[Aspects]].
- [[Patron Gods]] shape peoples, cultures, bodies, and inherited customs.
- [[Last]] witnesses truthful endings and protects the integrity of death.
- Minor gods, saints, ancestors, spirits, and divine beasts hold narrower domains.

A god's power depends on worship, domain, circumstance, symbols, and what kind of authority is being contested. A Patron defending its people may overpower a Crown's avatar; the Crown still retains deeper authority over its Aspect.
