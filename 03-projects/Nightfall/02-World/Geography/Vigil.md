# Vigil

The central city founded by survivors who kept watch for a dawn they feared would never return. Vigil holds the greatest [[Dawn Anchors]], the central temples of [[The Five]], and the command of [[The Watch]]. Its upper districts treat physical height as proof of spiritual and civic worth.
