# Facets

The Facets are the people of [[99-Wiki/entities/glass]], historically rooted in [[Meridian]].

## Inherited form

crystalline growths storing light and revealing emotional color. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Clothing and crystal display obligations. Hiding a flaw is worse than possessing one.

## Death custom

At death, focused light records a final image in crystal but preserves no final words.

A Facet can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
