# Confluences

A Confluence is not two damage types used together. It is a truth shared by two jealous [[Crown Gods]] that a divided mortal forces both to acknowledge.

| Aspects | Confluence | Shared truth |
|---|---|---|
| [[Ash]] + [[Void]] | Deepghost | What is forgotten approaches absence |
| [[Tide]] + [[Gale]] | Stormfront | Sea and sky meet across a moving horizon |
| [[Ash]] + [[Tide]] | Mistveil | Water preserves traces while mist conceals them |
| [[Ember]] + [[Tide]] | Crucible | Opposition creates transformation |
| [[Gale]] + [[Void]] | Riftwind | Movement requires empty space |
| [[Ember]] + [[Void]] | Blackflame | Hunger can consume even connection |

Unlocking one requires both Aspects, survival of [[Divine Friction]], discovery of the shared principle, and a deed meaningful to both Crowns.
