# Radiance

Radiance is the ordering condition that allows matter, memory, and identity to remain consistent. It is not a humanoid supreme god. Mortal souls express a small regenerative share of it as [[Luminance]].

[[Vigil]] survives because [[The Rise]] remains strongly aligned with Radiance. [[Dawn Anchors]] catch and redistribute that stability rather than manufacturing light from nothing.
