This document outlines critical updates and structural reconciliations made to the [[entities/nightfall|Nightfall]] world-building vault on September 17, 2026. The session focused on streamlining the project's cosmology by consolidating various theological and existential concepts, specifically integrating "Radiance" and "The Unlit" into the primary frameworks of [[entities/eidolon|Eidolon]] and [[concepts/darkness|Darkness]]. 

Significant attention was given to rectifying causal errors in metaphysical lore and addressing inconsistencies between the Roblox game codebase and the vault documentation regarding the canonical [[concepts/ring-order|Ring order]]. The notes also highlight a persistent architectural challenge regarding the project's folder structure. Furthermore, the entry identifies pending action items, including map point-of-interest validation and the alignment of world-building scale with technical constraints required for the Roblox implementation.
