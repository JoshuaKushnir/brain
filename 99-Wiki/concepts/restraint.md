# Restraint

## Definition

Restraint is a foundational attribute of the Void aspect, serving as the governing principle that enforces limitation, control, and the suppression of energetic or magical manifestations.

## Key Characteristics

- **Regulatory Nature**: Functions as a fundamental force of limitation, dictating the boundaries of action.
- **Suppression Capability**: Enables the dampening or complete halting of active magical and energetic processes.
- **Isolation Function**: Facilitates the separation of disparate elements within a system.
- **Structural Silence**: Inherently connected to the silence and stillness that defines the core nature of the Void.

## Applications

- **Containment**: Used to isolate targets or anomalous energy signatures to prevent interaction with the broader environment.
- **Stabilization**: Employed to halt chaotic or volatile magical processes by enforcing strict boundary conditions.
- **Boundary Enforcement**: Defines the physical or metaphysical limits within which a phenomenon or entity is permitted to exist.
