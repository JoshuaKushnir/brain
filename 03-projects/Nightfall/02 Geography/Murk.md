# Murk

The Third Ring, where the horizon is often lost. [[Redstep]], [[Mournwood]], and parts of [[Meridian]] lie here. Expeditions find advanced relics, major ruins, and unstable roads.

See also [[Map of the Rings]], [[Darkness]], and [[Luminance]].
