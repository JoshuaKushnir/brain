# Void

Void is the Aspect of separation, restraint, absence, and suppression. Its Crown is [[Orr]].

In combat, Void emphasizes phase evasion, silence, and mark-hunting. Loyal single-Attuned disciples gain the deepest stability; divided disciples risk [[Divine Friction]].

See [[Attunement]], [[Crownlight]], and [[Confluences]].
