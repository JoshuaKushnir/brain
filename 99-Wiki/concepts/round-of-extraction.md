# Round of extraction

## Definition

A round of extraction is a systematic iteration performed on a source file to identify, isolate, and catalog information entities and concepts. It serves as a procedural mechanism to ensure that dense or complex documentation is processed in manageable increments.

## Key Characteristics

- **Iterative Process:** Breaking down large volumes of information into sequential cycles to maintain accuracy.
- **Incremental Growth:** Each round builds upon the knowledge graph established by previous extractions.
- **Manageability:** Prevents information overload and cognitive fatigue during the indexing of extensive source materials.
- **Traceability:** Allows maintainers to track the evolution of the knowledge base through documented extraction stages.

## Applications

- Processing long-form technical documentation that requires multiple passes for comprehensive entity recognition.
- Refining the structure of a developing Wiki during high-volume data ingestion.
- Synchronizing updates across interconnected [[Wikilinks]] when source content is modified or expanded.
