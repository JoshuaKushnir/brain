# Hearth

Hearth is the common name for the First Ring. Refugees established the first shared fires, farms, and permanent homes here after the first [[Nightfall]].

It surrounds the foot of [[The Rise]] and supplies much of [[Vigil]]'s food and water. This dependence creates resentment: Vigil calls Hearth protected land, while many farmers call it taxed land.

Hearth is usually within a trained traveler's [[Dark Tolerance]], though mines, dead Dawn Anchors, and buried ruins can contain much deeper [[Darkness]].
