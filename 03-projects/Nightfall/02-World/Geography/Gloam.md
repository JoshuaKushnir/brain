# Gloam

The Second Ring and the last belt where ordinary sunlight consistently reaches the ground. The [[The Fen|Fen]] and many [[Moths]] lie here. Travelers first learn to measure Darkness rather than assuming daylight means safety.

See also [[Map of the Rings]], [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness]], and [[Luminance]].
