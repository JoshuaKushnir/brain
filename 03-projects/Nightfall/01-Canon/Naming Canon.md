# Naming Canon

Names should be short enough for natural dialogue and meaningful enough to imply history.

## Three layers

- **Common:** Vigil, Rise, Hearth, Gloam, Murk, Night, Rim, Dark.
- **Official:** First Ring, Second Ring, Third Ring, Fourth Ring, Fifth Ring.
- **Old:** Pre-[[entities/nightfall]] names preserved in ruins and divine records.

## Political language

- Vigil's officials say "the Five Rings."
- Crown priests say "the Five Descents."
- Crownless communities say "the Five Freedoms."
- Rim communities often call their homeland "the Edge."
- Nightspawn call [[Vigil]] "the Far Flame."

## Rule

A name must answer at least one question: what happened here, what is visibly here, who named it, or what does the name claim politically?
