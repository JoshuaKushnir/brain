# Dark Tolerance

Dark Tolerance represents the intensity of Darkness a person is accustomed to. Surviving slightly beyond the current limit raises it; overwhelming exposure usually breaks rather than trains a person.

Greater tolerance has costs: sunlight may become uncomfortable, safe areas may restore [[Luminance]] more slowly, and Nightspawn may treat the character as familiar.
