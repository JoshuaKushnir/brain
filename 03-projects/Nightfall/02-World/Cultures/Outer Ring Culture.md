# Outer Ring Culture

Night and Rim communities judge law by whether it preserves settlement survival.

- Anchor maintenance outranks private debt.
- Body recovery suspends ordinary feuds.
- Water and shelter cannot be denied during Darkness surges.
- Executions require known witnesses.
- Leaders who abandon occupied settlements can be challenged.

[[The Pact]] gives these customs political form.
