# Canon Decisions

## Locked direction

- The map is radial around [[Vigil]] and [[The Rise]].
- Darkness generally increases outward and downward.
- Names remain short and historically meaningful.
- Luminance is an exploration and identity resource, not mana.
- Crown Gods demand exclusive magical worship.
- Multi-Attunement is difficult, costly, and story-defining.
- Patron Gods create peoples but do not assign Aspects.
- Death uses Downed, Carry, Grip, Fall, Echo, and Return stages.
- Factions overlap morally and politically.

## Avoid

- Large bureaucratic cosmic hierarchies.
- Paired creation/destruction gods.
- Races functioning as locked classes.
- Automatic dual-Aspect power stacking.
- Death as consequence-free instant respawn.
