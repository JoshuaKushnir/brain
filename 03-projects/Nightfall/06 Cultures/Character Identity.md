# Character Identity

Character identity has five independent layers:

1. **People:** inherited form and Patron.
2. **Homeland:** language, habits, and survival knowledge.
3. **Faction:** active allies, enemies, services, and obligations.
4. **Aspect:** combat magic and Crown relationship.
5. **Oath:** chosen commitment above convenience.

No people is a locked class, personality, nation, or Aspect. This separation enables combinations such as a Moth Sounder Attuned to Void or a Returned Witness devoted to Tide.
