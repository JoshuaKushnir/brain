# Veyra

## Description

Veyra is one of the five [[entities/mareth|Crown Gods]] who serve as the primary deities within the pantheon. As a member of this divine group, Veyra governs one of the five distinct [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]] of the world. Followers of this deity are mandated to maintain their faith through a practice of exclusive magical devotion known as [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]]. This process ensures that the practitioner remains aligned with the specific energy governed by Veyra.
