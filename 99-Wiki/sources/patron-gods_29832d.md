## Core Content

This source outlines the divine entities known as the [[concepts/patron-gods|Patron Gods]], a collective of twelve figures who played a pivotal role in the history of the world during the event known as [[entities/nightfall|Nightfall]]. According to the text, these gods facilitated the survival of the twelve mortal peoples by establishing specific [[concepts/survival-covenants|survival covenants]]. The source serves as a primary reference for the foundational deities and their association with various regions and symbolic entities, establishing a theological framework for the mortal cultures that emerged in the wake of the [[entities/nightfall|Nightfall]] disaster.
