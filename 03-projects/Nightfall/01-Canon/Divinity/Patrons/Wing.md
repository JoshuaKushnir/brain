# Wing

Wing is the Patron of the [[Kest]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Wing changed mortal bodies so they could survive The Reaches, granting gliding and air sense.

## Character

Wing is joyful, reckless, and unreliable. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on migration, freedom, wind routes, promises measured by landings. Its danger is restlessness and abandonment of long duties.

## Divine relations

Wing may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
