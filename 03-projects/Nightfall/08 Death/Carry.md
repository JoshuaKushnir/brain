# Carry

Carrying a Downed ally or enemy limits movement and combat but relocates the rescue objective.

- Allies can evacuate someone to a [[Dawn Anchors|Dawn Anchor]].
- Attackers can separate a victim, demand ransom, or deliver a bounty.
- Recovering a corpse preserves equipment and reduces Return consequences.
- Body recovery fulfills many [[Witnesses|Witness]], [[The Lamps|Lamp]], and [[The Pact|Pact]] contracts.
