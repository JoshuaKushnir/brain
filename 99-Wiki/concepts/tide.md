# Tide

## Definition

Tide is a foundational aspect characterized by themes of continuity, pressure, grief, and the weight of things carried forward. It serves as a primary elemental or philosophical pillar within the system, focusing on the inevitable and cumulative movement of force.

Tide is regulated by the relevant Crown God, functioning as a formal, divine interpretation of magic rather than a simple elemental building block. Beyond continuity, pressure, and grief, it is also defined by the theme of obligation. As a classified Aspect, it remains distinct from restricted or forbidden magical forms such as [[concepts/marrow|Marrow]].
## Key Characteristics

- **Continuity**: Emphasizes the unbroken progression and persistence of forces over time.
- **Pressure**: Utilizes the weight and intensity of accumulated energy to exert influence.
- **Accumulation**: Unlike transient effects, Tide focuses on the build-up of potential and substance.
- **Directionality**: Prioritizes the flow, pull, and redirection of existing momentum.
- **Defensive Shaping**: Leverages the weight of carried burdens to create barriers or structural stability.

## Applications

- **Force Management**: Used in tactical scenarios to redirect incoming energy by folding it into existing flows.
- **Structural Reinforcement**: Applied to stabilize systems by anchoring them to the weight of historical continuity.
- **Grief Processing**: Acts as a psychological framework for integrating loss and trauma as a form of "carried weight" that provides momentum rather than hindrance.
