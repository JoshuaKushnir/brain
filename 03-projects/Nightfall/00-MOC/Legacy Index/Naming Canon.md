# Naming Canon

Names should sound inherited from daily life, disaster, labor, and faith—not invented as decorative fantasy labels.

## Three layers

- **Common names:** short words such as [[Vigil]], [[Hearth]], [[Murk]], [[Night]], and [[The Rim]].
- **Administrative names:** First Ring through Fifth Ring, used by [[The Watch]] and surveyors.
- **Old names:** pre-Nightfall words surviving in ruins and divine records.

Patron Gods use witness-names—[[Hearth]], [[Hammer]], [[Wing]], [[Lamp]], [[Kiln]], [[Spore]], [[Root]], [[Glass]], [[Hart]], [[Shell]], [[Face]], and [[Bell]]—describing how refugees first encountered them.

## Dialogue test

A successful name works naturally in speech: “born in Murk,” “down from the Rise,” “past the Rim,” or “the Watch closed Third.”
