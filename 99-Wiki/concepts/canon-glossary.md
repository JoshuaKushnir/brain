# Canon Glossary

## Description

The Canon Glossary is a foundational reference document providing a standardized set of terminology and narrative constraints essential for interpreting the metaphysical, spiritual, and physical framework of the *Nightfall* setting. It delineates the mechanics of existence, such as the stages of death and the nature of divinity, while establishing strict design philosophies to maintain internal consistency. By defining concepts like the resource-based nature of Luminance and the requirements for magical attunement, the glossary ensures that the world's history and moral landscape remain coherent. It serves as an authoritative guide that prevents common tropes, such as consequence-free respawn mechanics or restrictive race-based classes, while anchoring the setting’s radial geography around the central hubs of Vigil and The Rise.

## Definition

The Canon Glossary is a foundational reference document providing a standardized set of terminology essential for interpreting the metaphysical, narrative, and spiritual framework of the *Nightfall* setting. It delineates the mechanics of existence, the nature of divinity, and the processes governing life and death within the world.

## Key Characteristics

- **Standardization**: Establishes definitive meanings for complex metaphysical terms such as 'Aspect', 'Crownlight', and 'Undertide'.
- **Systemic Scope**: Maps the interplay between soul mechanics, identity, and environmental dissolution (e.g., 'Dark Tolerance', 'Luminance').
- **Lore Anchoring**: Serves as the authoritative source for defining the relationships between 'Patron' deities, the 'Rise', and their respective populations.
- **Structural Clarity**: Provides a governing lexicon for the laws of nature and spiritual conditions within the *Nightfall* universe.
- **Procedural Integrity**: Requires a one-paragraph summary for all entries and mandates that unresolved material be marked as a [[concepts/proposal|Proposal]] to ensure a clear distinction from established canon during development.
## Applications

- **Lore Reference**: Acts as a primary guide for researchers and writers to maintain consistency regarding the world's governing laws.
- **World-Building Framework**: Used to define the logical boundaries and interaction rules between magical and physical states in the *Nightfall* setting.
- **Narrative Interpretation**: Assists in the analysis of character motivations and existential struggles, particularly concerning identity retention against external forces.
- **Developmental Manifestation**: Canon status is often expressed through game mechanics, level design, quests, UI, animation, audio, and NPC behavior, ensuring that lore is integrated directly into the project's development and user-facing design.
