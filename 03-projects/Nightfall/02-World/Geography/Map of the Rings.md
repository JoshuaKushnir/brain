# Map of the Rings

The world is arranged around [[Vigil]] atop [[The Rise]]. The ring boundaries are ecological and historical belts rather than perfect circles.

| Order  | Common name                | General condition                    |
| ------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Center | [[Vigil]] and [[The Rise]] | Most stable sunlight and law         |
| First  | [[Hearth]]                 | Farms, roads, estates, early ruins   |
| Second | [[Gloam]]                  | Last reliable ground-level sunlight  |
| Third  | [[Murk]]                   | Lost horizons, dangerous expeditions |
| Fourth | [[Night]]                  | Sun visible mainly from peaks        |
| Fifth  | [[The Rim]]                | Last reliably mapped belt            |
| Beyond | [[The Dark]]               | Unstable geography and identity      |

The model is also vertical: caves in Hearth can reach Murk-level Darkness, while high terrain in Night may offer temporary relief.
