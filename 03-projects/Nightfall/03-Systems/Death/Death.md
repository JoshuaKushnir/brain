# Death

Death concludes a life while preserving that it happened. [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness]] instead disputes the person's identity, memory, and history.

The common teaching of [[Last]] is:

> Death keeps the name. Darkness changes it.

At zero Health, a character usually enters [[Downed and Holdfast|Downed]]. If not rescued, they Fall into [[Undertide]] as an Echo. [[Luminance]] remaining at death determines how coherent that Echo remains.

Death begins a [[Return]] sequence instead of a consequence-free respawn. Scars should change circumstances—equipment condition, routes, debts, memories, Oaths, divine favor, or Echoes—without routinely deleting long-term progression.

Permanent death is reserved for major NPC consequences, opt-in high-stakes modes, failed ascension, explicit bargains, and complete identity loss in [[The Dark]].
