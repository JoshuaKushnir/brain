# Luminance

Luminance is the soul’s regenerating resistance to unfamiliar [[99-Wiki/concepts/darkness]]. It is not Mana. It falls when local Darkness exceeds [[Dark Tolerance]] and recovers in familiar conditions, trusted company, ritual rest, or the range of healthy [[Dawn Anchors]].

## States

- **Bright:** clear senses and strong identity.
- **Steady:** minor whispers and distortion.
- **Dimming:** false sounds and altered paths.
- **Flickering:** memories fail and abilities distort.
- **Extinguished:** Darkness begins rewriting the character.

[[Crownlight]] reduces drain for a loyal single-Attuned disciple. [[Multi-Attunement]] weakens that protection.
