# Dawn Anchors

Dawn Anchors catch and redistribute alignment with [[Radiance]], creating pockets where [[Luminance]] can regenerate normally. [[The Lamps]] maintain them, while [[The Watch]] claims authority over them.

An Anchor can fail, be rationed, moved, sabotaged, or corrupted. Restoring an outer Anchor can change travel routes, settlement politics, and local Darkness rather than merely activating a checkpoint.
