# Hammer

## Description

Hammer is one of the twelve Patron Gods identified as central to the survival of the mortal peoples. Its name suggests a profound association with craftsmanship, industry, and martial prowess within the established mythology. The entity is cited as a vital figure alongside [[entities/hearth|Hearth]] and other deities during the tumultuous Nightfall period. Its influence is often invoked in contexts involving the physical construction and defense of society.
