# Masks

The Masks are the people of [[99-Wiki/entities/face]], historically rooted in [[The Quays]].

## Inherited form

slow bodily adaptation toward a chosen identity. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Public and private names have different protections. Removing a mask without consent is spiritual assault.

## Death custom

At death, the private name is spoken to Last and the public mask is broken.

A Mask can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
