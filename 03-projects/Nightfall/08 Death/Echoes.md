# Echoes

An Echo is the part of a dead person that reality still remembers. It carries the name, remaining Luminance, Crownmarks, Oaths, important memories, and spiritual impressions of gear.

Echoes may remain coherent, become hostile, split by Aspect, or be imitated by Darkness. [[Witnesses]] test whether a returning Echo is a continuation or replacement.
