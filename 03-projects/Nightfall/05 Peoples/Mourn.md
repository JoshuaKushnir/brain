# Mourn

The Mourn are the people of [[Spore]], historically rooted in [[Mournwood]].

## Inherited form

fungal symbiosis carrying selected ancestral memory. This gift changes survival and world interaction rather than assigning an Aspect.

## Culture

Families cultivate memory gardens. The dead may testify through preserved spores. Forgetting can be medicine.

## Death custom

At death, the body is planted and only chosen memories are cultivated.

A Mourn can come from another [[Cultures MOC|culture]], serve any [[Factions MOC|faction]], and undertake any [[Attunement]].
