# Bell

Bell is the Patron of the [[Returned]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Bell changed mortal bodies so they could survive scattered enclaves, granting Undertide awareness and second chances.

## Character

Bell is charming, merciful, and unable to accept endings. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on renaming after near-death, funeral bells, unfinished duties. Its danger is weak attachment to life and false Return.

## Divine relations

Bell may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[03-projects/Nightfall/01-Canon/Divinity/Patrons/Bell]] when death customs are violated.
