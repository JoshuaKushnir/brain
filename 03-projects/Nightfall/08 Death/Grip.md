# Grip

A Grip is a committed execution that forcibly separates soul from body. Damage, guard break, knockback, or forced movement can interrupt it before completion.

On completion, the victim falls deeper into [[Undertide]], immediate field revival ends, and the killer becomes attached to a [[Death Claims|Death Claim]]. Location, witnesses, factions, and Oaths determine consequences.
