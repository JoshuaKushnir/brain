# Eidolon

## Description

Eidolon represents the original ordering condition of the universe, serving as a foundational pillar alongside the [[Pale Wound]] and [[Darkness]]. Following recent cosmological consolidation, the concept of Radiance has been folded into Eidolon; specifically, Luminance is now understood as a share of Eidolon's former wholeness rather than a distinct metaphysical force. Mainstream doctrine holds Eidolon as an intact and singular entity. While historical theories suggesting that Eidolon divided into pieces known as 'Crowns' once existed, these interpretations have been relegated to the status of rare heresy. It functions as a primary reference point for navigating the history, causality, and metaphysical framework of the setting.
