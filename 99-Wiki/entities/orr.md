# Orr

## Description

Orr is a prominent member of the [[entities/crown-gods|Crown Gods]], specifically tasked with the governance of one of the five [[concepts/aspects|Aspects]]. The deity demands that all followers practice absolute, exclusive magical devotion to maintain their connection. This devotion is maintained through the rigorous ritual practice known as [[concepts/attunement|Attunement]]. Orr's influence is central to the theological structure governing the divine hierarchy of the realm.

Orr is identified as the Crown of the Void, serving as the representative authority or focal point for the aspect associated with restraint and silence.
