# Face

Face is the Patron of the [[Masks]]. Refugees first knew the god by this short witness-name during the first Nightfall.

## First gift

Face changed mortal bodies so they could survive The Quays, granting chosen appearance and identity resistance.

## Character

Face is compassionate, theatrical, and inconsistent. The god protects a way of life rather than an [[Aspects|Aspect]].

## Covenant

The covenant centers on public and private names, masks, chosen selfhood. Its danger is abandoning responsibility by changing identity.

## Divine relations

Face may cooperate or feud with the [[Crown Gods]] when Crown demands threaten the people. It also disputes with [[Last]] or [[Bell]] when death customs are violated.
