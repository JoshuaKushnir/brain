# Return

## Definition

Return is the metaphysical process of transitioning from the state of 'Undertide' back into the realm of the living. It represents a cycle of recovery from death, functioning as the fundamental undoing of mortality within the cosmology of the setting.

## Key Characteristics

- **Cyclical Rebirth**: Acts as a restorative event that reverses the process of dying.
- **Metaphysical Transit**: Involves a forced or intentional shift from the 'Undertide' state to physical existence.
- **Cosmological Significance**: Considered a major phenomenon that challenges the finality of death.

## Applications

- Used to describe the recovery or resurrection of individuals deemed to have passed into the 'Undertide'.
- Serves as the primary explanation for anomalies where death is not permanent.
