# Death Customs

- **Vigil:** names engraved where sunrise touches the Rise.
- **Hearth:** families preserve a coal from the funeral fire.
- **Gloam:** bodies face the last visible sunset.
- **Murk:** Sounders record the route so the Echo can navigate home.
- **Night:** death-lamps burn and bodies are never left unattended.
- **Rim:** the dead are tied to named objects because graves may move.
- **Dark:** survivors speak the name continuously until reaching mapped ground.

Lineage-specific rites appear in each people note.
